`TensorPathCoefficientCoherence.pathAction_heq` compares the direct
`VacuumTensorCoefficientMaps.pathCoefficientAction` along an arbitrary
upward reservoir path with `TensorWordCoefficientAction.wordAction` on
the reversed colour label. The comparison concerns the whole
supernatural endomorphism before regular-probe evaluation. Its weights
are the original formal-content and word-weight presentations already
identified by `TensorReservoirWords.path_weight`.

The proof follows the actual path. At the empty path, the recursive word
action is its prescribed endpoint and whole-Hom transport. At a cons
step, `ReservoirWord.word_cons` splits the literal whiskering into tail
whiskering followed by the prescribed `elvE`. The induction hypothesis
transports both the whole Hom and its coefficient map through the
proved intermediate weights. `wordAction_append_heq` removes only the
given whole-Hom append equality. No coefficient is evaluated, projected
to its even part, or commuted past another coefficient.

The paired consumer applies the comparison to the asymmetric two-odd
datum with a nonzero odd empty-word action, certified by its action on
a tensor containing the other odd colour. It also applies the comparison
to arbitrary labels, input components and mixed-parity coefficients.
The remaining observation step is the global word-chart coefficient
square; the path comparison supplies its direct-action input.
