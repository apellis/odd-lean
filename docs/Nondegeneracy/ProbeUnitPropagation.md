# Direct same-colour probe units

The direct unit proofs use no finite-dimensionality. For even nilHecke blocks the involution phi conjugates u to v. For odd blocks, writing a=u⁻¹ gives v*(-phi*a*phi-v)=u², hence a right inverse of v; the existing kernel propagation supplies injectivity and therefore a two-sided unit.

The actual cyclotomic word-corner theorem covers either parity. Induction in one fixed monochromatic corner proves every dot of the prescribed cofinal probe is a unit over any base ring; its first-dot premise is supplied by the actual cyclotomic relation. Rational odd and even consumers instantiate both the adjacent quadratic case and the last dot in a four-strand probe (power five in the even fixture, power three in the odd fixture). The distinct-colour step remains separate. Branch CI and integration reporting distinguish checked results from drafts.
