For the parity-coordinate assembly in Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14),
`ELVFullInductionSquareParity` proves that the selected extension,
restriction and source module isomorphisms are even. It also gives the
source and target constructors with their final endpoint isomorphisms
bound before specializing the categories.

`ELVContentBlockMatrixCharts` applies these constructors to the literal
multiset content inclusions. Its six `contentBlockMatrix{Source,Target,Value}Iso_{hom,inv}_mem`
results retain the chosen lower E/F, upper F/E and value charts, including
the final object equalities to the endpoint modules of `weightedMatrixMate`.
The forward and inverse module maps are even. The three `_total` theorems
evaluate the selected source, target and value charts, retaining their
specified endpoint maps and the strict restriction square. These are module
isomorphisms, so their inverse laws hold on all vectors and arrows.

Both producers and their paired concrete consumer pass strict scoped
checks. The consumer specializes both colour parities, parity-shifted
representable input modules, arbitrary truncation bounds and the zero
bound, and audits every declaration owned by the two producers.

columns rather than being discarded.
