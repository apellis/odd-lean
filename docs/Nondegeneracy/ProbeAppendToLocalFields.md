# The selected local fields at stored probe representatives

For the local relations in Brundan–Ellis (1.7)–(1.8) and (1.13)–(1.14),
`ELVProbeAppendToLocalFields` compares the actual evaluated fields at
arbitrary prescribed content representatives with their OLD module maps.
The edges are the selected `probeEvaluatedAppendTo` and
`probeEvaluatedRestrictTo` functors. Their right adjunctions retain the
ordinary balanced unit and counit.

`probeAppendToEqualCross` is the restriction of the specified negative
`inductionModuleCross` at those two append functors. It has the prescribed
colour parity. Its component and total comparisons retain every OLD
module arrow. The dot comparison and `probeAppendToDotPower_app` retain
each homogeneous component of every ordered power of the chosen dot.

`probeAppendToMate` is the ordered mate of this crossing and the two
selected right adjunctions. Its component comparison identifies the exact
OLD cup-crossing-cap; no new mate or compatibility premise is supplied.
These comparisons provide the target fields for attaching the reindexed
inverse entries. They do not by themselves supply that local inverse
record.

The paired consumer applies the comparisons to the rational two-colour
fixture with reordered mixed contents, both colour parities, arbitrary
power indices and homogeneous components, and an odd input module map.
