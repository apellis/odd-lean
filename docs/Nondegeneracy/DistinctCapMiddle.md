# The intermediate positive-height cap window

Brundan–Ellis Lemma 7.1 (7.3) treats the range
`0 < h_i(b) < d_ij`. The shifted height at `c = b + alpha_j` is
negative. This interval is not covered by the nonpositive-height cap
test or by the large-positive-height cup test.

Write `L_b`, `L_c` for the prescribed equal-colour local records. The
proposed factor proof precomposes the corrected mixed braid by
`-L_b.sigL`, retaining the actual source chart. A separated correction
monomial with source exponent below `h_i(b)` vanishes by
`L_b.sigL_capD`, regardless of its other two exponents. For source
exponents above that value, its final exponent is below the shifted cap
boundary and vanishes by `L_c.cupD_capL`. Every interior polynomial term
vanishes at one of these two endpoints.

Only the leading monomial with source exponent `h_i(b)` survives. Its
final exponent is `d_ij - 1 - h_i(b) = -h_i(c) - 1`. The identity
`capL = -(sigL ; capD h_i(b))` gives the raw value

    (-1)^(parity(i) * h_i(b)) * t_ij * (capL_b ▷ E_j ; leftUnitor).

The height-parity sign is retained; it is not silently absorbed into the
raw cap. Conversion to the canonical shifted left adjunction uses its
specified scalar unit. This is an intermediate raw cap identity, not
an additional hypothesis on the final theorem.

`RouquierDistinctCapMiddle` and its asymmetric two-odd consumer are
proposed and under verification. The consumer uses height 2 between the
asymmetric off-diagonal value 4, with all family and weight data
constructed. The source-chart identification and final normalized
all-height left-mate result remain separate attachments.
