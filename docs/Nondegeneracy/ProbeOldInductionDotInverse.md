# Old coend probe dot inverse

The probe-unit roadmap needs the prescribed signed dot on the untruncated old append coend. `ELVProbeOldInductionDotInverse` constructs its candidate inverse from the inverse ordinary appended dot, the scalar Hom action on unchanged old induced module values, and the original balanced quotient. The inverse uses the parity-square factor `sign k (D.parity i * D.parity i)`.

The ordinary inverse identities, scalar Hom identities, balance, signed inverse generator formula, and inverse-after-dot product have passed the scoped producer check. The dot-after-inverse product and resulting unit packaging remain under verification; the final canonical `probeOldModule` specialization and paired mixed/vacuum consumers are not yet checked. This draft does not establish the whole module or selected append-comparison tensor formula.

The proof uses scalar-evaluation closure of old induction, not scalar evaluation of a weighted coefficient truncation. The chosen ordinary content probe model and its dot, crossing, and adjunction comparison obligations remain unchanged.
