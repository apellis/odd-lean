# The induction-grid fields in the Rouquier parity target

For Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14), `inductionModuleAdjunction` packages the
existing even balanced induction/restriction adjunction in the canonical
module parity categories. `inductionModuleDot` retains the specified
induced dot; `inductionModuleCross` is the negative of the physical chosen
double-induction crossing. Its component and degree equations preserve
that minus in both moving parities.

`inductionModuleMate_eq_grid` identifies the entire prescribed Rouquier
cup-crossing-cap with `gridMateTransformation`. Thus the checked grid
inverse families have the required principal field, rather than a
separately assumed compatible mate. The consumer instantiates actual old
empty/one-letter content categories and their given dot and physical
crossing, including the ordinary right-adjunction triangles.

This comparison supplies forward fields for the old local Rouquier
construction. It does not assume or claim a complete family, a normalized
left cap, the local slide relations or the local inverse itself.
