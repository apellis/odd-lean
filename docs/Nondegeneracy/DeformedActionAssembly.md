# Deformed distinct-colour mate and action assembly

**Status: written proof of the full underlying SKM supercategory action over
the stated full-ELV coefficients; not a Lean implementation.** Integer-grading
preservation is not yet established. The mixed quadratic calculation in §6
and the corrected braid in `DeformedMixedBraid.md`, together with the explicit
zero components below, complete the presentation relations. The smaller
`RouquierFamily` interface alone does not do so. The same-colour matrix proof
is used without an additional kernel or pivot assumption.

Sources: KKO I, arXiv:1206.5933v2, Proposition 5.1 (pp. 26–27), Theorem 9.1
and equality (9.3) (pp. 46–47); KKO II, arXiv:1303.1916v2, §8.3 and Theorem
8.13 (pp. 68–69). Their cyclotomic hypotheses do not directly include the
noncentral full-ELV polynomials. The extension of (9.3) is proved below,
rather than inferred from the printed theorem.

## 1. Hypotheses and conventions

Work over a purely even commutative ring k with 2 invertible, with finite
compatible support in a symmetrizable Cartan superdatum and homogeneous
quiver-Hecke parameters with their prescribed invertible leading coefficients.
Let A and the monic homogeneous f_i be exactly those of
`DeformedIdealSum.md` §1. Thus for an odd colour, writing
C(z)=z^m+χ₁z^(m−1)+…+χ_m,

* N=2m>0: f(x)=C(x²)−xd(C(x²)−χ_m)/x² and dχ_m=0;
* N=2m+1: f(x)=(x−d)C(x²), without the relation dχ_m=0;
* N=0: f=1 and no d is introduced.

Distinct d_i are independent exterior directions subject only to their own
specified ELV relations. A is not replaced by a field or by its even quotient.
For content β put B_β=R_A(β)/(F₁), where (F₁) is the two-sided ideal.
It is homogeneous even when its colour components have different parities.
All parity involutions below include A. Fix a cutoff weight Λ with
N_i=⟨h_i,Λ⟩; if the lattice does not realize these coordinates, realizing or
extending it is an additional weight-data task, not a consequence of this note.

Use left modules and signed-left, ordinary-right maps of superbimodules.
Our E is induction and F restriction, so weights are λ=−Λ+β, not Λ−β.
The upward dot is R_x(z)=(-1)^(|x||z|)zx. The crossing is R_τ for distinct
colours and −R_τ for equal colours. This is the normalization of
`CyclotomicAction/GlobalRouquierData.lean`, not a freely chosen Mackey map.

## 2. Deformed equality (9.3)

For n≥1 write H=R_A(n+1), R=R_A(n), L=R_A(n,1), and f=F₁. These
height algebras are finite sums over the fixed colour support. All products
below implicitly sum over the matching word idempotents. L preserves the last
colour; it is generated by R and the last dot. Let I=HfH and I_R=RfR.
For i≠j and e_i=e(n,i), e_j=e(n,j), the assertion is

    e_i I e_j = e_i I_R H e_j + e_i H I_R e_j.                 (1)

Here the two copies of R are the first n positions, with the appropriate
last-colour corner units. No common scalar parity for the sum f is assumed.

Set T_a=τ₁⋯τ_a (T₀=1), U_a=τ_n⋯τ_a. The accepted parabolic
normality proof in `DeformedIdealSum.md` gives

    I = Σ_(a=0)^n H f T_a.

It uses b f_l=f_l ρ_l(b) in the first-colour parabolic, where at odd l
ρ_l(x₁)=x₁−2d_l and ρ_l on coefficients and tail generators is total parity
to power p(l)N_l. Both the translation and the odd-cutoff coefficient sign
are essential. Consequently, since T_a∈L for a<n and I is an ideal,

    I = H f L + H f T_n L.                                  (2)

The affine left-coset PBW decomposition is H=Σ_(a=1)^(n+1) L U_a,
with U_(n+1)=1. Expand the H immediately before fT_n in (2).
For a≥2 every crossing in U_a avoids position 1. On a first-colour
component, it supercommutes with f_l with the sign given by the TOTAL
parity of f_l. Thus these summands are in L f H. This statement follows
termwise for the entire f; it does not require f to be central. The remaining
summand is L U₁ f T_n L. We have therefore proved

    I = H f L + L f H + L U₁ f T_n L.                        (3)

Each term on the right is contained in I, which proves the reverse inclusion
as well. The last dot supercommutes with every first-position f_l, so
HfL=HfR and LfH=RfH. This removes the extra polynomial factor of L
without asserting that the old cyclotomic ideal is principal.

Finally, T_n moves the last colour j into the first position; f does not
change the word; U₁ moves that same colour back to the last position.
Both external L factors preserve the last colour. Hence

    e_i L U₁ f T_n L e_j = 0  (i≠j).

Taking the corner of (3) proves (1). We never commute f through τ₁,
never invert a crossing, and never discard a same-colour divided-difference
term. The long double-crossing expression is killed by its support BEFORE
any quadratic or braid reduction. This argument covers odd N, even N and
N=0. At n=0 the mixed corner is zero, as is the smaller-content tensor
kernel; there is no old f to use in (1).

## 3. The specified crossing isomorphism and its inverse

Fix |β|=n, β'=β−α_i+α_j, i≠j, and put

    D=B_(β−α_i), B=B_β, B'=B_β', C=B_(β+α_j), e=p(i)p(j),
    M=B' e(β−α_i,j) ⊗_D e(β−α_i,i) B,
    N=e(β',i) C e(β,j),
    M♯=B' e(β−α_i,j) ⊗_D Π^e(e(β−α_i,i)B).

Absent contents mean zero corners. The left D-action on the second shifted
factor is d·π^e b=π^e φ^e(d)b; its right B-action is unchanged.
There is an even superbimodule isomorphism, with integer degree accounted for
by the displayed source shift,

    ρ : q^(−(α_i|α_j)) M♯ → N,
    ρ(a⊗π^e b)=a τ_n b.                                    (4)

Before quotient this is the exact crossing map of KKO I Proposition 5.1,
not an unspecified decomposition. Its balance follows from
cτ_n=τ_nφ^e(c) on the old n−1 positions, including homogeneous c∈A.
It is ordinary left and right linear in the shifted coordinates.

For completeness, its affine inverse is the crossing-coset PBW inverse:
choose R(n) as the direct sum of τ_a⋯τ_(n−1) x_n^r R(n−1),
1≤a≤n, r≥0, with word idempotents and coefficient order fixed. Map each
such tensor basis summand by insertion of τ_n before its final right factor.
The images are the nonidentity cosets in the PBW expansion of e_i H e_j.
Sliding x_n^r past this final τ_n is a signed substitution x_n↦x_(n+1),
with invertible sign; the two colours at this crossing are distinct, so there
is no divided-difference remainder. The identity coset is zero because i≠j.
Thus coefficient extraction in these crossing-adapted cosets is the inverse:

    ρ_aff^(-1)(Σ a_l τ_n b_l)=Σ a_l⊗π^e b_l,                (5)

where the sum is first put in those fixed PBW tensor coordinates. Formula
(5) is not a claim that an arbitrary crossing expression is unique.

Extend this PBW isomorphism to A by tensoring its explicit inverse; flatness
is unnecessary. Quotient on the left and right by I_R. Balanced tensors with
both outer quotients identify with the tensor over D, since the smaller
cyclotomic ideal maps into both outer ideals. Equality (1) says exactly that
the corresponding quotient of the affine mixed corner is N. Hence (4)
and the descended (5) are inverse bimodule maps. In particular this is an
isomorphism of the actual quotient kernels, not merely an equality of ranks.

## 4. Identify the cup–crossing–cap mate

Let s:M→N be the prescribed tensor unit–crossing–counit mate, using the
unshifted right adjunction E⊣F. On homogeneous tensors it is

    s(a⊗b)=(-1)^(e|a|) a τ_n b.                             (6)

Indeed the unit inserts the corner identity, the two induction factors
contract to a, their signed crossing contributes (-1)^(e|a|), and the
counit multiplies b on the right. All structural maps are even. This is the
same computation as the accepted same-colour comparison, except that there
is NO equal-colour minus sign. A final arbitrary module factor is unchanged.

Define the canonical parity transport

    J:M→M♯,  J(a⊗b)=(-1)^(e|a|)a⊗π^e b.

The balancing sign for replacing a by ac is (-1)^(e|c|), precisely the
sign from moving c across π^e. J and its inverse are right B-linear and
signed left B'-linear of parity e. Consequently

    s=ρJ,                 s^(-1)=J^(-1)ρ^(-1).              (7)

Explicitly, s(za⊗b)=(-1)^(e|z|)z s(a⊗b) and
s(a⊗bc)=s(a⊗b)c. The inverse has the same signed linearity. Its parity is
e and its degree is +(α_i|α_j), opposite to the degree of s. In even shifted
notation the isomorphism is q^(−(α_i|α_j))Π^e M≅N. Tensoring these
right-linear maps with ANY left B-supermodule gives inverse natural
transformations. Neither projectivity nor flatness of that module is used.
This proves the requested distinct-colour prescribed mate for full ELV.

## 5. Concrete all-module target and rank-one assembly

The ambient scalar ring is k, not the odd ring A treated as an ordinary
commutative scalar ring. For each β use all Z×Z/2-graded left B_β-modules.
A homogeneous morphism u of parity r satisfies
u(bm)=(-1)^(r|b|)b u(m). Take finite sums of homogeneous morphisms when
forgetting grading. This is a k-linear supercategory; its parity completion
and integer shifts are explicit grading shifts. In particular homogeneous
maps are A-superlinear, with their required sign on odd coefficients.

A (B',B)-kernel P acts by P⊗_B−. On homogeneous module maps u its action
is p⊗m↦(-1)^(|u||p|)p⊗u(m). A signed-left, ordinary-right kernel map
v acts by p⊗m↦v(p)⊗m. These formulas descend through balancing:
the sign contributed by an intermediate b is exactly the signed left-linearity
sign of u or v. For two kernel maps the horizontal tensor formula is
(v⊗w)(p⊗q)=(-1)^(|w||p|)v(p)⊗w(q). Its two possible composites differ
by (-1)^(|v'||w|), proving super interchange on pure tensors. Associators
are reassociation of tensors and unitors are multiplication, all even; their
coherence reduces to equality on pure tensors. This constructs the required
k-linear two-supercategory of these module categories and tensor functors,
without pretending that odd coefficients are even scalars.

Use P=B_(β+α_i)e(β,i), Q=e(β,i)B_(β+α_i). The appended-strand map
descends because the old first-position relations lie in the new ideal.
The unit sends m to e⊗e⊗m, and the counit sends p⊗q⊗m to pqm.
The two triangles are pe=p and eq=q. These are the even right-adjunction
triangles, with NO q-shift on restriction. A convention using a normalized
shifted restriction has to transport the adjunction; it cannot silently use
this unit and counit. No reverse adjunction or `LeftZigzags` is asserted.

The common contraction of iterated induction is multiplication of its
kernels. Under it dots and crossings are the signed right multipliers above;
the tensor signs are exactly those of super interchange. For homogeneous
multipliers a₁,…,a_l applied in that order the formula is

    R_(a_l)⋯R_(a₁)(z)
      =(-1)^((Σ|a_r|)|z|+Σ_(r<s)|a_r||a_s|) z a₁⋯a_l.   (8)

This follows inductively by adding |a_(l+1)| times the current parity.
It supplies the passage through all induction associators, not just a
calculation on a regular module with unspecified functor identifications.

For a fixed i, square-zero, equal-colour braid, and both signed dot slides
follow from the affine rank-one relations and (8), with T=−R_τ. The
three-crossing sign is the same on both braid words; the extra minus occurs
three times on both sides. The two slide constants are +1 precisely with
this equal-colour minus convention. Homogeneity gives dot parity p(i),
crossing parity p(i)p(j), and even unit/counit.

The accepted `DeformedMackeyBoundary.md`, `DeformedBoundaryDivision.md`,
`DeformedRouquierMatrix.md`, and `DeformedMatrixComparison.md` supply the
same-colour prescribed inverse matrices at ALL heights. In particular for
a=⟨h_i,Λ−β⟩=−m<0, the kernel window has entries
H_rk=EX^rψ_k=φ_(r+k+1)−φ_(r+k)t, zero below the antidiagonal and γ^(-1)
on it. The rows act semilinearly on coefficients; the specified output
parity involution removes that semilinearity in its RIGHT-TWISTED target.
This is already a unit-triangular inversion, not a residual nonvanishing
condition. Use s=−F̄J, cups d_k j_k, and caps
 e_k φ^(p(k+1))EX^kJ exactly as in that comparison. Do not tensor a naked
φ with an arbitrary module. The transported caps are right-linear.

Thus the written construction supplies every field of `SKM.RouquierFamily`:
`obj`, `E`, `dot`, `cross` and their parity fields, `F`, `adj`, `square`,
`braid` (equal colour), and `loc`, including `slide_left`, `slide_right`,
`invPos`, `invNeg` and all homogeneous inverse components. Bijectivity of
the even shifted matrices makes their inverse components homogeneous with
the required parity. Their matrix product identities give all `ff`, `fg`,
`gf`, `gg` and `total` fields, rather than just an abstract splitting.

At a weight λ with several β satisfying −Λ+β=λ, take the product of the
module categories over those β; induction reindexes by β↦β+α_i and
restriction is its componentwise right adjoint, using zero on absent
predecessors. A weight with no β has the zero category. This retains
root-kernel coordinates and supplies all weights of this lowest-weight
family, not arbitrary nonzero test objects at every external weight.

## 6. Global relations and the underlying presentation action

`GlobalRouquierData.lean` defines `globalRouquierData : RouquierData`; it
is not a structure named `GlobalRouquierData` and does not implement ELV.
Its coefficients are ordinary polynomials `a : I → Polynomial k` satisfying
`CyclotomicPolynomialParity`. `RouquierFamily` only adds rank-one relations;
it has neither a mixed quadratic/braid field nor a distinct-colour mate
field. Accordingly §5 plus §4 is stronger than its fields in one direction,
but is STILL NOT by itself a full SKM action.

The upward relations in the *transported super-opposite presentation* are
tautological from the quotient and (8). Identifying that presentation with
the fixed SKM parameters is not tautological. For a permitted mixed monomial
u^r v^s the necessary parameter transport is

    Q^act_ij[u^r v^s]
       =(-1)^(p(i)p(j)+p(i)⌊r/2⌋+p(j)⌊s/2⌋) Q^alg_ij[u^r v^s]. (9)

This is the involution `parameterTwist`. It preserves support and leading
units; an action for specified Sc must start with algebra parameters
`parameterTwist Sc`, not reuse Sc without change. Distant supercommutation,
distinct dot slides, equal-colour relations, and the mixed quadratic follow
from (8) and (9). For a braid with no repeated distinct outside colour,
both words have the same multiset of crossing parities and the same number
of equal-colour minus signs, so their uncorrected relation also follows.

Here is the mixed quadratic comparison, including the reversed labels.
Put p=p(i), q=p(j). On E_i E_j the appended tuple is (j,i); write x₁
for its j-dot and x₂ for its i-dot. For a nonzero coefficient c_(r,s)
of Q^Sc_ij, the support laws give pr=qs=0 separately modulo two. The
prescribed chronological right-j then left-i dotted layers act as

    (-1)^(p binom(r,2)+q binom(s,2)+pqrs) z x₁^s x₂^r
      =(-1)^(p floor(r/2)+q floor(s/2)) z x₁^s x₂^r.

The dependence on total |z| vanishes by pr+qs=0. The two signed crossings
instead give (-1)^(pq) z Q^S_ji(x₁,x₂), with S=parameterTwist Sc.
Simultaneous label/exponent symmetry gives the coefficient of x₁^s x₂^r
in that polynomial as

    (-1)^(pq+p floor(r/2)+q floor(s/2)) c_(r,s).

The crossing-square factor cancels pq, proving the exact quadratic relation
term by term without commuting dots. This covers both leading terms and
all interior terms. For disconnected colours the polynomial is the SINGLE
constant t_ij, not a sum of two constant leading terms; its twist is
(-1)^(pq)t_ij, which cancels the crossing-square sign as well.
The named parameter twist preserves scalar symmetry, homogeneous support
and leading units and is involutive, as in `ParameterTransport.lean`.

The remaining corrected three-strand comparison for i,j,i with i≠j is
proved in `DeformedMixedBraid.md`, retaining the original SKM coefficient
order. In the precise notation of `SKM/Presentation.lean`, it is the vanishing
of the image of `Rel.braidEq i j λ hij`, using the tensor functors above,
algebra parameters `parameterTwist Sc`, and prescribed signed crossings.
For each coefficient c_(r,s) of Q^Sc_ij, its correction is the sum over
0≤l<r with coefficient

    (-1)^(p(i)(p(j)+l)) c_(r,s),

and the ordered dotted diagram has layers: l dots on the right i, then s
dots on j, then r−1−l dots on the left i. This order must be retained.
The LHS is the first–second–first crossing word minus the
second–first–second word, with their changing colour labels, exactly lines
264–278 of that definition. The leading t_ij term is the s=0 instance.

`BraidCorrection.lean` proves the algebra relation and `BraidOperators.lean`
proves the uniform triple-operator sign, but neither alone identifies this
corrected relation after the scalar involution and the position/order reversal
of the right-action convention. The absence of a mixed field in
`RouquierFamily` cannot fill this logical gap. Formula (8) reduces it to a symbolic sign identity for arbitrary exponents,
proved in `DeformedMixedBraid.md` by reversing positions and reindexing the
correction without commuting dots. No centrality hypothesis is added.

For the generators outside a finite inverse matrix, explicitly set
dcup i n=0 when h_i(λ)≤n, and dcap i n=0 when −h_i(λ)≤n.
These maps discharge dcupZero and dcapZero and are homogeneous in every
required degree. At height zero the two inverse-crossing definitions agree
by uniqueness of inverse. All five positive and five negative inverse
matrix relations are the corresponding products of the specified matrix
and its inverse, not extra assumptions.

Thus the quadratic equal/connected/disconnected cases, both dot slides,
equal and distinct uncorrected braids, corrected braid, right-adjunction
zigzags, distinct-colour inverse identities, and all same-colour matrix
identities are satisfied. Distant commutations come from super interchange.
Together these give the written action of the full UNDERLYING SKM
supercategory by its generators and relations.
No additional projectivity, trace, field hypothesis, cyclotomic freeness or
left adjunction is required for this all-module conclusion. The full-ELV
Lean category, functors, comparisons and presentation factorization remain
to be implemented. Fusion, arbitrary-weight nonzero detection, normalized
regular bubbles and nondegeneracy are separate obligations and are not
consequences claimed here.

## 7. Integer grading is not yet transported

The construction above uses unshifted restriction with degree-zero unit
and counit. It therefore defines the underlying supercategory action, not
yet an action preserving the standard SKM integer grading. In that grading
the cup and cap have degrees d_i(1+h_iλ) and d_i(1−h_iλ) at their
respective labelled weights; the distinct-colour inverse crossing has
degree zero. The raw distinct-colour mate in §4 instead has degree
−(α_i|α_j), with the inverse of opposite degree.

To promote the claim to a standard integer-graded action, specify the
weight-dependent integer shifts on restriction and transport the adjunction
and every inverse component through them. Checking the homogeneous degree
of the upward quadratic and braid relations is necessary but does not
supply those shifts. Parity grading and all underlying relations proved
above are unaffected by this remaining obligation.
