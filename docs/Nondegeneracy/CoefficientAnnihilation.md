# Coefficient annihilators under old E and F

The power ideal in [SwappedNondegeneracy, §2](SwappedNondegeneracy.md)
is generated by powers of positive even coefficient generators. It is
different from the weighted coefficient ideal defining `TruncatedCategory`.
`ELVCoefficientAnnihilation` proves the required preservation statements
directly on old modules, without identifying these ideals.
The producer and concrete consumer pass scoped Lean checks with warnings
as errors and both pinned compatibility settings. All 12 owned declarations
pass the standard axiom allowlist audit. Full CI remains the integration gate.

For a full ELV coefficient a, `coefficientEndomorphism(v,a)` is its
literal coefficient-category endomorphism at the old word v. The signed
box-product composition gives

    e_v(a) composed with e_v(b)=e_v(b*a).

This is `coefficientEndomorphism_comp`: categorical composition reverses
the coefficient multiplication order. For two odd coefficients this order
cannot be exchanged without its exterior sign. A covariant representable
acts by postcomposition, retaining this exact convention.

For homogeneous a of parity p and every old arrow f:v->w,
`coefficientEndomorphism_slide` proves

    f composed with e_w(a)=e_v(a) composed with twist_p(f).

Actual right append preserves these coefficient endomorphisms.
Define `KillsCoefficient(M,a)` to say that e_v(a) acts as zero on M for
every v. Then `restriction_killsCoefficient` proves preservation by actual
old F. `induced_killsCoefficient` proves preservation by actual old E:
on a balanced generator [h tensor m], slide a through h and use balance
to make it act on m, where it vanishes. Full coefficient parity is retained.

`induced_killsEvenPower` specializes this result to every generator a^L
of an even coefficient power ideal. Applying these results to each
generator gives closure of its annihilator class under E and F.
Furthermore `moduleMap_evenCoefficient` proves that every supernatural
module map, including an inhomogeneous one, commutes with each even
coefficient. It applies to both literal maps of any chosen isomorphism;
no alternative inverse is selected.

The concrete consumer uses the actual weighted-empty representable pulled
back to the old category for the connected two-odd-colour datum. At cutoff
four, weighted bound four kills the square of the first elementary even
coefficient, and the actual E/F preserve that square's annihilator.
This is a concrete module testing the generic preservation result; it is
not asserted to be the paper's power quotient. The consumer also checks
even-coefficient commutation with the identity plus the chosen odd
induction-dot transformation.

## Remaining quotient bridge

The literal power quotient/category and its comparison with the written
reservoir are not constructed here. If T is the even power ideal and
every generator has weight at least N0, then T is contained in W_N0.
Finite dimensionality of A/T supplies R with W_(R+1) contained in T.
Thus the quotient arrows are weighted(R+1) -> power(T) -> weighted(N0).
Bounded injectivity for the larger ideal W_N0 implies it for T. It does
not follow in the reverse direction after imposing an additional quotient.

The existing arbitrary-T `TransformedIdeal` detection theorem still needs
identification with the actual evaluation kernel. The concrete full factor,
its chosen generators and inverses, quotient comparison, finite coefficient
bounds and normalized vacuum evaluation remain separate constructions.
