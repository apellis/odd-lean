# Prescribed finite matrices on changing-category induction grids

Source: Brundan--Ellis (1.13)--(1.14), and DeformedMatrixComparison (4), (5).

For J:C→A and K:A→B, `gridUnitMatrixFamily` uses the canonical source shifts
of Ind_J Res_J N and N. `gridCapMatrixFamily` uses the canonical target
shifts of Res_K Ind_K N and N. The first field is the independently given
`orderedGridMateComponent`; the remaining fields are the already specified
shifted unit/dot columns and dot/counit rows. All entries are even.

The given entries assemble into `gridUnitMatrixModuleMap` and
`gridCapMatrixModuleMap`. Their evaluated maps are literally the named
finite components, which are even on the actual pointwise family grading.
The first and dotted fields have public raw-total formulas before concrete
category or module specialization. These are actual module maps natural in
all coefficient-category arrows, including inhomogeneous ones.

The strict producer and paired consumer pass, with all 35 owned declarations
audited against the repository allowlist. The consumer checks both moving
parities on the literal old and ordinary changing-content grids, the first
and dotted fields, whole module maps/components, inhomogeneous identity-plus-
dot output arrows, and both zero-size vacuum matrices. For a same-colour
crossing its degree p² is explicitly normalized to p in ZMod 2.

This specifies the prescribed matrix objects and maps. Invertibility and
comparison under selected E/F charts remain subsequent obligations; no
inverse or entry-compatibility assumption is introduced here.
