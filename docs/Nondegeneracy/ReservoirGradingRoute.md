# Reservoir grading for `CentralInvertible`: route assessment

**Status: assessment of formalization routes, with the abstract part checked in Lean
(`OddMath/SKM/RouquierGrading.lean`, see §4).** It concerns the two inputs of
`SwappedHomInversion.md` §4 that come from the integer gradings (G6)–(G8) of
`DeformedGradedAction.md`:

* (N) joint nilpotence of the contracted reservoir closures
  `b_(s,r)=cupD_W(r);R_W;capD_W(s)`, of degree `2d(r+s+1)>0` (its (8)–(10), (12));
* (J) membership of the `r>j` window contractions `v_R(r)a_R,j`, `b_R,j u_R(r)`
  in the ideal `J` generated by positive-degree closed reservoir coefficients (its (7)).

## 1. Which objects carry an integer grading

* **The presented 2-category.** `SKM.gdeg` (`SKM/Grading.lean`) assigns the degrees of
  Brundan–Ellis, arXiv:1701.04133v2, §1 "Gradings", to all generators of `pres D Sc`,
  including the inverse entries (`lcross` 0, `dcup i n` `dᵢ(h-1-2n)`, `dcap i n`
  `-dᵢ(h+1+2n)`), and `isHomogeneous_gdeg` proves every relation homogeneous under (1.31).
  The generic `StringDiagrams.Presentation.isInternal_homDeg` then makes every Hom space of
  the presented category an internal direct sum of degree pieces;
  `QuiverHecke.presentedGrading` packages this as a `GradedSupercategory`.
* **Quiver Hecke superalgebras.** `QuiverHecke/Grading.lean` grades the quiver Hecke
  supercategory by the same degrees.
* **Abstract graded 2-supercategories.** string-diagrams-lean has `GradedSupercategory`
  (Brundan–Ellis, arXiv:1603.05928v3, Def. 6.1), with degree projections `dproj` and
  `inv_mem_degree`, and `GradedTwoSupercategory` (Def. 6.2).
* **The factor actions do not.** The cyclotomic factors (`globalPiRouquierFamily`) live in
  `PiSCat`, whose 2-morphisms are supernatural transformations of functors between
  categories of supermodules. Only the parity grading is constructed there (the induced
  tensors of `CyclotomicAction/IntertwinerGrading.lean` carry parity, not degree). There is
  no integer grading of the cyclotomic quotients, of the induced kernels, or of the
  natural transformations, and no finiteness (bounded degree) theorem for cyclotomic
  quotients. The full-ELV deformed factor of `SwappedActionAssembly.md` F3 is not
  constructed at all.
* **The probe is ungraded on purpose.** `SwappedHomInversion.md` §1 imposes no grading on
  the specialized unit-dot probe; its cyclotomic polynomials have nonzero constant terms, so
  its quotients are not homogeneous. Since probe and reservoir share the ambient `B` of a
  `ProbeReservoir`, a global `GradedTwoSupercategory` instance on `B` is the wrong
  interface: the grading must live only on the 2-morphisms between reservoir objects.

## 2. Candidate routes

(a) **Graded reservoir interface, then the facts abstractly.** Put an integer grading on
the Hom categories `W.obj μ ⟶ W.obj ν` between reservoir objects (a `GradedSupercategory`
on each, whiskering additive, unitors and associators of degree `0`), require the forward
generators to have the degrees of the table in `DeformedGradedAction.md` §5 (dot `2dᵢ`,
crossing `dᵢdᵢⱼ`, unit `dᵢ(1+h)` at its source weight, counit `dᵢ(1-h)` at its target
weight), and require bounded-above support of `End(𝟙_μ)` at every weight. Then:

* (G8) is a theorem, not an input: in any graded supercategory a `SuperSumInvTo` or
  `SuperSumInvFrom` matrix inverse of homogeneous entries has homogeneous entries of the
  negated degrees. This is the argument of `DeformedGradedAction.md` §4 ("a bijective
  homogeneous map of degree (0,0) ... has homogeneous inverse"), done entrywise with the
  degree projections; no biproduct objects are needed.
* the mate has degree `0`, the dotted cups/caps (G6) have degrees `c_r=d(h+1+2r)`,
  `e_s=d(1-h+2s)`, so `b_(s,r)=η_r;f';ε_s` has degree `2d(r+s+1)` and the boundary
  contractions `boundaryPos m n`, `boundaryNeg m n` of `SuperRouquierLoc` have degree
  `2d(n-m)`, `2d(m-n)`; this is (J);
* a product of `n` positive-degree elements of `End(𝟙_μ)` has degree `≥ n`, hence vanishes
  once `n` exceeds the upper bound; this is (N) at the reservoir. Transport to the probe Hom
  category is by post-whiskering, which is multiplicative; the probe needs no grading.

This is the written argument with its two written inputs ((G1)–(G8) and the upper integer
support `M_β` of the finite homogeneous truncation) stated as one interface on the
reservoir. It is engineering only.

(b) **Grade the concrete reservoir.** Construct an instance of (a) for the reservoir the
final assembly uses. For the written reservoir this is the content of
`DeformedGradedAction.md` applied to the full-ELV deformed factor, which must first exist;
the grading must be put on the induced kernels (graded bimodules) and transported to the
supernatural transformations, and the upper bound comes from the finite homogeneous
truncation. For the present undeformed `globalProbeReservoir` the same work is needed, and
in addition the cyclotomic ideal is integer homogeneous only when every `aR i` is a monomial
`x^(N_i)` (coefficients have degree zero), and the upper bound needs a finiteness theorem for
cyclotomic quiver Hecke superalgebras that is not formalized. This is faithful but large.

(c) **Through the presented 2-category.** Every coefficient in (N) and (J) is the image of a
homogeneous closed diagram of `pres D Sc` (a dotted bubble or a boundary contraction), whose
degree `isHomogeneous_gdeg` already controls. This needs a 2-functor from the presented
2-supercategory to `B` built from a `RouquierFamily` with `RouquierMulticolour`, which does
not exist (string-diagrams-lean only has maps between presentations), and it still needs the
reservoir to kill high degrees, i.e. the upper bound of (b). It does not remove (b).

(d) **Finite-dimensionality alone, or a direct argument for the instance.** Not sufficient:
finite dimensionality of `End(𝟙_μ)` does not make a particular central element nilpotent;
positivity of its degree is the reason, so some integer grading is unavoidable.

## 3. Faithfulness and size

* (a) and (b) are what the written proof does. Nothing in them is new theory. The written
  (G8) argument is proved rather than assumed. (c) differs from the written route (it
  grades through the presentation instead of grading the action) but is standard; it is not
  needed. No route requires a decision by the maintainer.
* (G6)–(G8) are consistent with the Lean-checked `gdeg` table and its homogeneity theorem;
  nothing found suggests they are false.
* The upper bound is a property of the **truncated** reservoir: the deformed coefficient
  ring has positive-degree generators in every degree, so the untruncated deformed factor is
  not bounded above. The written proof works on finite homogeneous reservoir quotients
  (`SwappedHomInversion.md` §1, §8); the final assembly must use those.

Estimated size: (a) 2–3 PRs (interface and inverse degrees; closures and nilpotence;
transport to the Hom-sector coefficients in the form the `CentralInvertible` proof
consumes). (b) for the deformed reservoir: after that factor exists, roughly 10–20 PRs
(integer grading of induced kernels and of their supernatural transformations, the
truncation and its bound, generator degrees). (b) for the undeformed cyclotomic reservoir:
similar, plus cyclotomic finiteness, and it is not the written reservoir.

## 4. What is checked

`OddMath/SKM/RouquierGrading.lean`:

* `SKM.RouquierGrading W sy`: the interface of (a), without the upper bound;
  `RouquierGrading.BoundedAbove`: the upper bound at every weight.
* `SuperSumInvTo.f'_mem_degree`, `g'_mem_degree`, and the `SuperSumInvFrom` versions:
  homogeneous inverse entries in any `GradedSupercategory` (the (G8) argument).
* `RouquierGrading.mate_mem` (degree `0`), `dotCap_mem` (`e_n`),
  `dotCup_mem` (`c_n`), `invPos_f'_mem`, `invPos_g'_mem`,
  `invNeg_f'_mem`, `invNeg_g'_mem` ((G8)).
* `RouquierGrading.closure_mem`: `b_(s,r)` has degree `2d(r+s+1)`;
  `boundaryPos_mem`, `boundaryNeg_mem`: degrees `2d(n-m)`, `2d(m-n)` (so the
  `r>j` contractions of (7) have positive degree).
* `RouquierGrading.BoundedAbove.exists_foldr_eq_zero`: a composite of more than `M` elements of
  `End(𝟙_μ)` of positive degree vanishes; with `closure_mem_degree` this is (N) at the
  reservoir.

Open: the transport of (N) and (J) to the coefficient ideal on the Hom-sector windows of a
`ProbeReservoir` (post-whiskering), and route (b).
