# Prescribed content entries under canonical parity coordinates

Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) and DeformedMatrixComparison (4), (5) require the
actual first mate and dotted unit/cap fields, with their canonical parity
coordinates. `ELVContentMatrixEntries` compares those given fields through
the selected untruncated content E/F charts.

The four equations are `oldContentUnitEntry_none_square`,
`oldContentUnitEntry_some_square`, `oldContentCapEntry_none_square` and
`oldContentCapEntry_some_square`. They are whole linear-map equalities,
for arbitrary modules, output objects, finite sizes and dotted indices.
The first entries use the original crossing with its degree normalized
by p²=p. The dotted entries use exactly the same dot powers and ordinary
adjunction unit or counit as the original old matrix.

The proof decorates the established unshifted mate, unit-column and cap-row
squares with canonical source or target parity coordinates. Generic
column/row composition lemmas are applied before the content data are
substituted. The pointwise coordinate charts are the previously selected
`oldContentUnitSlotEquiv` and `oldContentCapSlotEquiv`; no compatibility,
chart-evenness or invertibility hypothesis is added.

The strict source and paired consumer cover both moving parities, every
finite count including zero, and arbitrary vectors on a mixed-content
representable module. The consumer audits every declaration owned by the
new module. Full CI remains the integration gate.

The scope is full-coefficient `OldContentModules` at the explicit upper
content `i :: ν`. Coefficient-truncated content, absent-predecessor content
and original weight transport remain separate. Whole finite assembly and
attachment of the prescribed inverse are subsequent steps.
