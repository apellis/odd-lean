# Natural dot inverse on the evaluated old probe target

SwappedNondegeneracy §3 and SwappedActionAssembly F2 require a unit dot
on the probe factor category. `ELVProbeEvaluatedDotUnit` constructs an
isomorphism of the whole induction superfunctor between the checked
scalar-evaluated old module categories. Its forward is the prescribed
signed balanced dot transform, and its inverse has the same colour
parity, including at odd colours.

Every evaluated object gets its native module-dot isomorphism from the
existing actual scalar-quotient inverse on the original old coend.
Homogeneity and supernaturality then assemble those isomorphisms into a
natural inverse on the entire input category. No component inverse,
finite-dimensionality, or uniform exponent is assumed.

`probeEvaluatedInductionDot_total` identifies the exact native module
component; `probeEvaluatedInductionDotIso_hom` retains the whole selected
forward. `probeEvaluatedInductionDot_isUnit` is a unit in the endomorphism
ring of the whole evaluated induction superfunctor, rather than a
statement restricted to representable input objects.

The producer and paired concrete consumer pass strict scoped checks and
an audit of all 30 owned declarations. The consumer checks both colour
parities, mixed content and vacuum, the exact forward component, both
inverse products and inverse parity.

Weight-product assembly and representative transports remain separate.
No unit is asserted on arbitrary old modules outside scalar evaluation.
