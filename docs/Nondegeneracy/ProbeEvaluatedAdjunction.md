retain the physical module maps. `probeEvaluatedInductionDot_native_total`

`oldContentInductionDotComponent`, rather than introducing another dot.

scoped checks. All 26 owned declarations pass the axiom audit. Both
colour parities and the exact native dot, unit and cap maps are exercised.
