# Corrected supernatural mixed braid

Current scope and verification are maintained in [STATUS](STATUS.md). Any original
topic-head pending checks below are historical; they do not override the consolidated
status or establish completion of the full conjecture.

Both whole paths are vertical compositions of the independently induced prefix
and suffix crossing families. In the repeated-outer case, the general right
path is Iij and the general left path is Jii.

The correction polynomial is formed in the actual triple-superfunctor
endomorphism algebra from the independent first, middle and last dot
transformations. It keeps the ordered monomial
C^(r-1-q) B^s A^q and the full sign
zsign(parity(i) * (parity(j)+q)). Its coefficients are the original scalar
parameters, while the functor and module category use parameterTwist.

Total evaluation at a module and then at a target object is an algebra map.
It preserves the ordered sums, scalar multiples, products and powers, so the
whole equation reduces to the previously proved component mixed braid.
Total evaluation detects equality at both levels without a homogeneity
hypothesis on the resulting polynomial.

Transport to weight-product successor blocks and packaging the remaining
Rouquier action fields are separate.

Evaluated powers are typed as endomorphisms of the actual SVec object before
forming the polynomial; an untyped Hom expression does not supply the End
multiplicative structure to instance synthesis.
