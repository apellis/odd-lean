# Entries of the cyclotomic matrix ideal

This is the reverse ideal comparison needed by
[AppendMackeySource](AppendMackeySource.md), section 4.

The algebra is the actual finite-support Hom matrix algebra Hn at positive
height, with the existing cyclotomicIdeal generated by first-dot relations.
For x in that ideal, every actual (target, source) matrix entry lies in the
ordinary morphism ideal cycHom at those endpoints.

The proof takes the k-linear span of single matrices whose entries lie in
cycHom. Composition closure of cycHom makes this span closed under arbitrary
left and right matrix multiplication. Every cyclotomic matrix generator
belongs to the span, so the whole actual ideal does. Evaluating an entry
of the span lands back in cycHom.

Together with single_mem_cyclotomicIdeal, this yields

    single(t,s,f) in cyclotomicIdeal  iff  f in cycHom(s,t).

This identifies the relevant actual ideal entries without a second matrix
presentation, monicity, a parity hypothesis on the polynomials, or tensor
flatness. The finite-colour hypothesis is the one already needed for the
unital Hn algebra.

The proposed implementation is CyclotomicMatrixEntries. Concrete connected
odd-data consumers use an inhomogeneous polynomial, actual generators with
arbitrary right and left matrix factors, and the single-entry equivalence.
Source and consumers await checking. Quotient-corner equivalences and the
height-tensor inverse are separate consumers of this comparison.
