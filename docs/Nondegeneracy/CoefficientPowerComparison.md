This supplies the categorical directions of the coefficient quotient

`coefficientWeightedIdeal(N)` is the two-sided ideal in the actual full
ELV coefficient algebra generated by the literal coefficient basis
monomials of weight at least N, including odd monomials.
`coefficientWeightedIdeal_le_annihilator_iff` proves that annihilating
this ideal is exactly the existing `KillsWeightedCoefficients` condition.
Thus the comparisons use the same ideal and descent maps as the actual
`TruncatedCategory` modules.

Let T be `coefficientPowerIdeal(c,L)`. The two containments have different
module-functor directions:

* From T contained in W_N, `weightedToPower` pulls an actual weighted
  module back along the existing old-to-truncated quotient, then regards it
  as a T-annihilated object. It does not impose another quotient on a vector
  space.
* From W_N contained in T, `powerToWeighted` descends a T-annihilated old
  module to the actual weighted category by the existing `weightedModule`
  construction, on unchanged vector spaces.

Both functors are additive, linear and parity preserving. Their component
vector spaces and all homogeneous components of every mapped module map
are literal identities in the chosen coordinates. Consequently functorial
transport retains the prescribed hom and inverse of every supplied
isomorphism, along with both inverse products.

For the paper's sandwich W_(R+1) contained in T contained in W_N0, the
actual module directions are therefore

    weighted(N0)-modules -> T-modules -> weighted(R+1)-modules.

These are contravariant to the quotient-algebra arrows. No injectivity
after a further quotient is inferred.

The consumer tests both functors on the connected two-odd-colour
weighted-empty representable, including the literal odd parity comparison
and the inverse component of its chosen isomorphism. As an auxiliary
