# ELV quotient and vacuum generator comparison

**Status: proposed written obstruction and quotient calculation; not a Lean
proof of the vacuum identification.** The untransported comparison required
by `RegularVacuumComparison.md` §§2 and 4 does not follow from the deformed
left-module action. There are two distinct problems: the named normalized
Hom construction puts the reservoir on the other side, and the signed dot
changes the cyclotomic relation when its coefficient coordinates are kept
fixed. The latter already prevents the proposed coefficient-preserving
cyclic map, not merely an isomorphism. Neither conclusion alone refutes
the existence of a fused action or faithful detection.

In particular `RegularVacuumIdentification` requires only k-linear
coordinates and a constructed reservoir coefficient action; it does not
require preservation of the raw factor scalars. The obstruction below is
to the untransported strengthening, not to that structure. Bounded
coordinate recovery likewise does not require a global quotient isomorphism.

All claims below refer to the definitions listed here, not to an identification
of the ordinary-polynomial Lean action with the full ELV action. The full
coefficient action used for comparison is the written one in
`DeformedActionAssembly.md` §§1,5–6 and `DeformedGradedAction.md` §§1–2.

## 1. Which Hom orientation is actually defined?

`RouquierHom.homPre(E)` sends Z to E;Z; `homPost(E)` sends Z to Z;E.
Here semicolon means chronological composition. Their homogeneous
transformations have components respectively η ▷ Z and Z ◁ η.
Neither definition reverses a module category or an algebra.

More specifically, `RouquierHomContent.lean` defines

    homWeightSector T W muP muM = Hom(T(-muP), W(muM)),
    homContentPreE(T,W,i,h) = homPre(T.E(i,h)),
    h : -nuP + alpha_i = -muP.

`HomSectorTransport.md` §1 names T the reservoir and W the probe. Its
reservoir upward functor is L_A=homPre(E_A); its probe upward functor is
L_B=homPost(E_B) followed by the content parity shift. The normalized
adjunction in `RouquierNormalizedHomAdjunction.lean` changes the reservoir
**downward** functor and its cup/cap components; it does not replace L_A by
postcomposition or turn its E into restriction.

In contrast, `RegularVacuumComparison.md` §3 places the probe at the input
of V, kills probe induction by precomposition, and retains reservoir
postcomposition. These are different typed Hom objects. They cannot be
identified by an associator or by the common tensor carrier.

This is visible without any nonzero higher probe block. In the unreversed
lowest-weight tower T(-Lambda+beta), E carries beta to beta+alpha_i.
Starting at the zero-content reservoir input, precomposition with E would
have to come from content -alpha_i. That component is zero. It is not the
postcomposition induction from content zero to alpha_i used for the regular
row. More generally, precomposition decreases this input content, while
postcomposition increases the output content. Keep the formal content
index: coincident external weights do not identify these components.

Thus the support argument in `RegularVacuumComparison.md` is a valid
argument for its stated orientation, but is not yet an argument for the
normalized Hom family just cited. Swapping the two factors also reverses
the cutoff difference; `ProbeReachability.md` explicitly fixes reservoir
minus probe. An opposite/reversed-family transport, with weight labels and
generators specified, is necessary before either support equation can be
used in the other construction.

## 2. The exact quotient correspondence that does hold

Write H_A(s,t) for `CoefficientH` Hom and I(s,t) for
`ELVRelationSpan cutoff s t`. Its multiplication is exactly

    (a tensor f);(b tensor g)
      = (-1)^(|a||g|) (ba tensor (f;g)).                 (1)

In particular this is not the unsigned tensor algebra in diagram order.
Fix a formal content beta and take the finite set of its words. Form the
matrix algebra K_beta with entries H_A(s,t), with multiplication in
endomorphism order: an entry f:s→t followed by g:t→u contributes f;g
to the product g f. Denote its word idempotents by e_t.

Let L_beta be the two-sided ideal generated by exactly the entries in
`ELVGenerator`: coefficient-ideal elements at every word, the odd
`elvCyclotomic` at its own first colour, and the even `evenCyclotomic`
at its own first colour. Then

    e_t L_beta e_s = I(s,t).                            (2)

Proof in both directions: a generator of I is a;r;b. Embed its three
factors in their matrix positions; it is a product in L_beta. Conversely
expand a finite sum of matrix products defining L_beta and cut by e_t,e_s.
Every nonzero entry is exactly a;r;b of the defining span. Affine diagrams
preserve content, so the apparent intermediate words of the same length
but different content contribute zero. This argument uses all crossings,
not just a first-strand corner. It uses no PBW freeness or flatness over A.

Consequently the following are explicitly inverse k-linear maps:

    e_t(K_beta/L_beta)e_s → ReservoirQuotient(s,t),
        [matrix with entry h at (t,s)] ↦ [h],
    [h] ↦ [matrix with entry h at (t,s)].                (3)

Equation (2) proves both descents; taking an entry and reinserting it proves
both inverse identities. They preserve composition (1). The regular
projective column (K_beta/L_beta)e_t has component at s equal to C(t,s),
where C=H_A/I; the row e_t(K_beta/L_beta) has component at s equal to
C(s,t). In particular it is the **row**, not the left-module induction
column, that has the representable coordinates P_t(s)=C(s,t).

At content zero the same calculation gives the coefficient quotient.
At every content the coefficient ideal is literally
`SYM.elvCoefficientIdeal`. For odd cutoff 2m it includes delta_i chi_(i,m),
along with the high elementary generators; at cutoff zero it kills delta_i
as well. At odd cutoff 2m+1 there is no extra delta_i chi_(i,m) relation.
No exterior direction is discarded in (2)–(3).

Append J_i is well defined on this quotient. On representatives append an
identity i-strand on the right, keeping the coefficient in its original
position. Identity strands are even, so (1) is preserved. A coefficient
relation appends to the same coefficient relation; a first-position
polynomial appends to the identical polynomial with a longer tail. Thus
J_i(a;r;b)=J_i(a);J_i(r);J_i(b) lies in the new relation span. Linearity
proves descent on the entire span, including the zero-content case.

This also verifies the balancing for the representable induction kernel
B_i(s,u)=C(s,J_i u): for a:u→u', b:s→J_i u, and m:u'→t,

    (b;J_i a);J_i m = b;J_i(a;m).                       (4)

Hence the contraction [b tensor m]↦b;J_i m and inverse
h↦[h tensor 1_t] are well defined on its coend. The first composite is
h;1=h; the second uses the balancing relation to replace
[b tensor m] by [b;J_i m tensor 1_t]. This calculation is valid for the
exact quotient, all formal contents, and odd coefficients. It does **not**
identify that kernel with the already specified deformed action kernel.
The latter starts with R_A(parameterTwist Sc), not with K_beta declared
by its coefficient-category composition.

## 3. Opposite transport does not remove the dot sign

The deformed action uses left B-modules and signed-left homogeneous maps.
Its induction kernel is B' e with its ordinary right B-action. The inspected
Lean prototypes are `inductionCorner` in `SuperInduction.lean`,
`inductionSuperDot_apply` in `SuperDot.lean`, and
`compositionSuperCrossing_apply` in `SuperCrossing.lean`. Together with
`globalActionSignedCross`, their formulas are

    D_x(z)=(-1)^(|x||z|) z x,
    T_ij(z)=epsilon_ij (-1)^(p(i)p(j)|z|) z tau,
    epsilon_ij=-1 if i=j, and +1 otherwise.              (5)

These are also the prescribed full-ELV formulas in the assembly. Their
parameters and coefficients must be transported, not read off from a row
module having the same underlying vectors.

Explicitly, let B^sop have multiplication
b^sop c^sop=(-1)^(|b||c|)(cb)^sop. A left B-module M becomes a right
B^sop-module by

    m · b^sop = (-1)^(|b||m|) b m.                      (6)

Associativity follows since the exponents on the two sides are
|b||m|+|c|(|b|+|m|) and |b||c|+(|b|+|c|)|m|.
A signed-left map u of parity r becomes ordinary-right-linear: both
u(m·b^sop) and u(m)·b^sop equal
(-1)^(|b|(|m|+r)) b u(m). The inverse transport is
b m=(-1)^(|b||m|)m·b^sop, so both composites are the identity.
In particular B e becomes the row e B^sop, not the row e B with its old
multiplication. Its vector transformation D_x is still (5).

For every odd dot, direct induction gives

    D_x^n(z)=(-1)^(n|z|+n(n-1)/2) z x^n,
    D_x^2(1)=-x^2.                                    (7)

The induction step adds |z|+n to the exponent. Neither an even associator,
an integer grading shift, nor the ordinary inverse of (6) cancels (7).
Crossings have the analogous pairwise parity signs under composition,
and the equal-colour minus in (5) must also be retained. The parameter
involution in the assembly addresses affine mixed relations; it does not
by itself identify the cyclotomic coefficient ideal after (7).

## 4. A quotient obstruction, already at an odd cutoff of size two

Take one odd colour, cutoff N=2, over k=Q. This tests a necessary local
restriction of an all-colour coefficient-preserving comparison; it is not
offered as a proof of an all-content positive comparison.
The full coefficient ring is

    A=Q[chi] tensor Lambda(delta)/(delta chi).

The written factor polynomial is
f=x^2-x delta+chi=x^2+delta x+chi. The exact coefficient-category
polynomial also has entries

    1 tensor x^2 + delta tensor x + chi tensor 1,       (8)

by `elvMonicCoefficient` and `deformedCyclotomic`. Notice that no change of
ideal has been made in writing (8).

Suppose the proposed comparison is coefficient-preserving, sends the cyclic
unit to the cyclic unit, and intertwines the specified D_x with
postcomposition by 1 tensor x. Such a bimodule comparison descends under
the coefficient specialization delta=0. The coefficient relation delta chi=0
is respected by this specialization. Both underlying one-strand quotients
then have relation x^2+chi=0. In the factor, however, (7) gives

    D_x^2(1)=chi,

while in the required target

    post_x^2([1])=[x^2]=-[chi].                         (9)

If kappa preserves the coefficient action and cyclic unit, applying kappa
to the first identity and using dot intertwining gives simultaneously
kappa(chi)=chi and kappa(chi)=-chi. This forces 2chi=0 in the target,
which is false: the underlying algebra Q[chi,x]/(x^2+chi) is Q[x], with
chi=-x^2. Equivalently evaluation x=1, chi=-1 kills the relation and not
2chi. This last evaluation is only an underlying-algebra nonvanishing
witness, not a parity-preserving probe or a graded action.

Thus even a cyclic coordinate map with these coefficient and dot laws is
impossible for the untransported factor coefficients. This does not assume
flatness to specialize an isomorphism: tensoring mutually inverse maps
preserves their inverses, and coefficient-linear intertwining identities
remain identities after base change. If only a coordinate map is asserted,
the same argument uses its descended identities and no inverse at all.
It does not rule out a *different, explicitly transported* reservoir
coefficient action, which is precisely what still has to be specified.

A candidate monomial sign correction illustrates the issue concretely:
dot intertwining forces x^n↦(-1)^(n(n-1)/2)x^n on the cyclic orbit.
If it fixes chi, it sends x^2+chi to -x^2+chi, not into (x^2+chi).
It therefore has no induced map on this exact quotient. This is an ideal
obstruction, not just an omitted Koszul sign on a tensor representative.

## 5. Narrow repair and remaining mathematical obligation

The square already justified by (2)–(4) is

    representable induction tensor --postcomposition by g--> new tensor
                 | contraction                         | contraction
                 v                                     v
           C(s,t) ----------------post_g------------> C(s,t').

It is not yet the square with the factor's transformation (5) on top.
To replace that top arrow, the existing route needs a single explicit
transport of the **specified** factor action to this coefficient category:

1. Specify which reversed/opposite family occupies each side of the Hom
   object, with the exact weight difference. This must reconcile §1 with
   the normalized pre/post functors, not simply rename reservoir and probe.
2. Write the generator and coefficient map from the transported algebra
   with `parameterTwist Sc` to the matrix category in §2. Prove equality of
   the images of the entire two-sided ideals, in both directions, at every
   formal content. Preserve the even-cutoff delta_i chi_(i,m) relation.
3. Transport the induction corner and its balanced actions through that
   map, then check (5), including equal-colour crossing signs, against (1).
   In the specialization of §4, keeping the dot and the cyclic unit already
   requires chi_alg↦-chi_coord (or the equivalent explicit pullback of
   coefficients). Fixing both is impossible. This necessary sign is not
   an all-colour coefficient transport theorem.

This is a correction to the comparison statement, not a proposal to change
the ELV ideal silently or to replace the factor action. Until that transport
is provided, `RegularVacuumIdentification` cannot be filled by the raw
contraction. Nothing here claims normalized bubbles, a completed fusion,
nonzero higher probe blocks, or nondegeneracy. The parity-graded invertible
dot probe may be used conditionally as supplied; no integer-graded probe
hypothesis is introduced. The reservoir's independent positive integer
grading and all its odd coefficients remain intact.
