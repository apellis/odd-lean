# The actual negative pre-cap on the regular probe

This is the inverse-power endpoint comparison needed by
SwappedHomInversion §3 (4) and SwappedActionAssembly F2.

`vacuumPreInverseDot_source` evaluates powers of the actual whiskered
inverse of the chosen whole-weight dot. It uses the same
`vacuumPreSourceIso` as the checked cap and raw-dot charts. Its target
is the corresponding power of `Ring.inverse shiftedPreDot`.

`vacuumPreNegativeCap_native` then evaluates the actual
`homPreNegativeCap`: the raw inverse-dot power followed by the
prescribed normalized pre-Hom counit. It becomes the native inverse
shifted pre-dot power followed by `shiftedPreCap`. Neither map is
replaced by a moment formula.

The inverse comparison follows from the forward raw-dot square and
both actual inverse identities. The native raw dot is a unit by its
given odd polynomial. The paired consumer uses both odd colours of the
asymmetric fixture, every negative exponent, the nonzero regular
vacuum vector, and a nonzero shifted cap.
