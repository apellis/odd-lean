# Chosen weight principal inverses in content coordinates

The comparison needed by [ReservoirClosureDegree](ReservoirClosureDegree.md)
starts with the literal `elvRouquierFamily.loc` inverse, whose existence
was constructed for Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14).

`elvChosenCapPrincipal_coordinates` and
`elvChosenUnitPrincipal_coordinates` identify the chosen principal at each
supported output `blockSucc β` with a canonical content-target inverse,
through the prescribed source and target object equalities. They do not
unfold the choice used to construct the local record. Instead,
`elvCapPrincipal_coordinates` and `elvUnitPrincipal_coordinates` prove the
same equations for any inverse of the prescribed finite forward matrix.
The already checked mate and dotted row/column comparisons, together with
the finite inverse identities, determine its principal uniquely. The
count identities retain the original weight height in either orientation.

The equality is between whole content-module arrows, for every input
family. It needs no symmetrizer, integer grading, inverse-comparison
hypothesis or independent-root assumption. The paired consumer checks
both moving parities and positive, negative and zero heights, and applies
both chosen equations to an odd singleton representable whose exterior
vector is independently proved nonzero. All 46 owned declarations are
audited against the standard axiom allowlist.

[ContentPrincipalInverseCoordinates](ContentPrincipalInverseCoordinates.md)
continues from these content-target inverses to weighted coordinates.
The later weighted-to-OLD and balanced-kernel equations are still needed
to identify the chosen reservoir closure with its positive-degree raw
coefficient. This module alone does not prove closure degree or central
matrix invertibility.
