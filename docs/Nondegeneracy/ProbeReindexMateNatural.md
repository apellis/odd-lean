# The whole OLD module mate comparison

For Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14), the coend mate comparison must hold as a
module map, including both homogeneous components at every target word.
`oldReindexLoopModuleHom_mate` in `ELVProbeReindexMateNatural` is the
module-map form of `oldReindexLoopLinear_mate`.

`oldReindexLowerLoopModuleHom` is the chosen lower append-adjunction
comparison evaluated after lower restriction. `oldReindexUpperLoopModuleHom`
is the upper append-adjunction comparison mapped by upper restriction.
Their total evaluations are exactly `oldReindexLowerLoopLinear` and
`oldReindexUpperLoopLinear`. The negative ordered crossing mate is the
original cup-crossing-cap, with neither its sign nor its bending maps
replaced.

The proof uses total-component extensionality and the checked single-coend
square. Its paired consumer uses both colour parities on reordered mixed
contents and on a parity-shifted evaluated regular probe. The statement
compares whole module maps; equality of the chosen dotted unit/counit rows
and attachment of the local inversion record remain distinct steps.
