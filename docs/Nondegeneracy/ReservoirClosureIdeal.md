# Chosen closure membership in the content coefficient ideal

This is the next attachment after the chosen closure degree calculation,
for condition (J) of SwappedHomInversion §4. The source and paired consumer
are proposed until their complete dependency chain passes strict checks.

At a fixed output content, the reconstructed coefficient of either chosen
principal closure has degree `2*d_i*(r+s+1)`. Realizing that coefficient
recovers the whole exact-content closure, so it lies in the corresponding
`contentModuleCoeffGrading` degree. Since the symmetrizer is positive,
that degree is at least one. The generic coefficient ideal transport then
places its post-whiskering action, with arbitrary morphisms on either
side, in level one of `contentModuleCoefficientIdeal`.

Only source coefficients carry the integer grading. The surrounding Hom
morphisms need no degree or parity restriction. The contentwise ideal's
joint nilpotence bound is inherited from the already constructed source
coefficient bound, not from a common bound on the entire weight fibre.

This membership supplies the closed principal coefficient part of the
central residual argument. The literal mixed-bend finite expansions,
window-boundary contractions and supported matrix comparisons must still
be connected before asserting central invertibility.
