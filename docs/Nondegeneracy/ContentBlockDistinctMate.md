# Distinct mates on canonical truncated content modules

This proposed continuation of Brundan–Ellis (1.12) uses the actual
multiset-indexed `ContentModules` on `TruncatedCategory`. The previously
checked global truncated inverse and selected full-content charts supply
its proposed proof route. No flatness or additional inverse assumption
is introduced.

`ELVFullInductionSquareSource` fixes the chosen full induction square,
its input-module isomorphism, the whole raw coend map, its bijectivity,
and its even tensor formula before specializing categories. It also
transfers an independently proved formula for the chosen input vector.
This generic producer passes a scoped strict check; its paired concrete
consumer remains part of the proposed batch.

`ELVContentBlockDistinctMate` retains four append functors with a common
upper content `(β + [j]) + [i]`, the positive crossing, and the selected
source and target full-inclusion charts. The lower functors use the
literal `contentAppendTo` with their canonical target and reflexive
content proof, matching the instances of the selected charts. The raw
source coordinates retain the inclusion functor objects and commuting
square coefficient transports. The complete canonical source tensor
comparison still needs its scoped check.

`ELVContentBlockDistinctInverse` proposes the whole content/global mate
square. The source and target bijectivity are supplied by the chosen
chart isomorphisms; the global mate bijectivity is the proved PBW result.
The final module and input-natural inverses are proposed applications of
the generic homogeneous rectangular mate constructor, with exactly
`i ≠ j`. Their forward maps are the independently prescribed positive
cup-crossing-cap, and their inverse degree is `parity i * parity j`.

The concrete consumer is proposed source: mixed and distinct odd colours,
parity-shifted representables supported at the input content, whole
roundtrips, odd input-map naturality, an exterior coefficient together
with its identity sum, and the empty content at zero cutoff. The final
canonical mate square, inverse, and this consumer are not yet checked.
Original-weight transport remains subsequent to this batch.
