# The prescribed probe row in the existing left-adjunction route

At negative local height h=-P, `SuperRouquierLoc.capL` is exactly the
last inverse row, `(invNeg ...).g' (P-1)`. Its `cupD_capL` equation is the
finite Kronecker window on the actual ordinary right unit and powers of
the actual induction dot. `appendRightUnitPower_total` identifies those
fields on the chosen native regular probe without any compatibility
hypothesis. The given finite unit-column inverse then forces the raw
local row to be `ProbeVacuumCap.cap`, by `local_capL_total`.

This comparison is conditional on supplied local Rouquier data, not a
construction of those data. The lower edge is arbitrary; the upper edge
is the exact chosen native induction/restriction pair and right
adjunction, with the same signed dot. No assumed identification of a cap,
no left zigzag hypothesis, and no complete Rouquier family is used.

For two adjacent local records, square-zero crossings and their braid,
`negativeLeftAdjunction` constructs the existing shifted left adjunction.
Its unit remains the upper raw inverse cup. The native counit has its
fixed factor `c(h)⁻¹=(-1)^σ`, with σ=(P-1)p. Accordingly
`localLeftCounit_total` identifies it with `koszulSign σ σ • cap`, not
with the raw normalized row when σ=1.

The output-shifted pre-Hom counit has the second diagonal Koszul factor
of `HomSectorTransport.md` (4). `localLeftCounit_preSign_total` and
`localLeftCounit_preCap_eq` retain and cancel both factors, giving exactly
the earlier cap on the canonical `E ◁ ζ(FH)` source coordinates. No scalar
is moved from the cap to the unit, and no alternative adjunction gauge
is chosen. The transpose Z and mixed-slide identification still require
the actual local records and their upward square/braid equations.

The consumer checks every right unit/dot power on the regular probes at
odd cutoffs 2 and 4, their nonzero native cap, its negative normalization,
and the double-sign cancellation. It also type-checks the raw row
comparison for supplied native local input. It does not construct that
input or assert an unconditional native left adjunction.
