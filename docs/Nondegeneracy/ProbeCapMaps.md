This source snapshot is proposed and has not passed its paired consumers.

last coefficient cap on the native F/E regular probe, with target the
original regular T₀, proves component surjectivity and nonzero module cap,
and gives a surjective ground row through the original empty coordinate.

The paired consumer is unchecked. Its intended data are the asymmetric
two-odd-colour rational probes at levels one and two. Homogeneous degree,
chosen scalar inverse-row binding, and the final mixed slide remain open
in this snapshot. No failing source is exported by the library.
