# Boundary coefficients through an even upper-loop chart

Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) retain the finite dotted-unit inverse through
the chosen coordinates. `SKM.RouquierBoundaryTransport.ofIso` transports a
constructed boundary coefficient record along an even isomorphism of its
upper loop. The two upper adjunctions may have different target objects;
the central region, scalar ring, parity profile and finite window agree.

The intermediate inputs are the loop isomorphism, its even parity, the
chosen target dot parity and the comparison of every actual dotted unit
with the target dotted unit. Every transported row is exactly the inverse
chart followed by the original coefficient. The total and entry equations
follow by conjugation and cancellation of the chart. Window zero is
included. These are explicit coordinate premises, not an assumed inverse
or action relation; concrete applications must construct the chart and its
unit comparisons.

The exact producer and concrete consumer pass strict checks with warnings
as errors and both compatibility settings. All five owned declarations
pass the axiom allowlist. The consumer uses identity charts on the actual
missing-colour OLD matrix in both moving parities, including nonempty
mixed content and zero cutoff. It verifies the transported total, entries,
coefficient parities and preservation of every given row. The transported
top cap returns the independently nonzero full PBW ground vector. A
nonzero regular vacuum vector is retained at zero cutoff as well.

The selected-probe upper-loop chart and its dotted-unit comparison remain
application-specific obligations. The generic transport theorem does not
supply those comparisons as an action-family hypothesis.
