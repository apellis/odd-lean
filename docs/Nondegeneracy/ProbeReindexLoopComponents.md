# Components of the chosen evaluated loop comparisons

Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) uses the selected lower and upper loop
comparisons to transport the finite inverse. The declarations
`probeReindexLowerLoopSuperIso_hom_total_native` and
`probeReindexUpperLoopSuperIso_hom_total_native` identify their total
components, after forgetting scalar-evaluation certificates, with
`oldReindexLowerLoopModuleHom` and `oldReindexUpperLoopModuleHom`.

The former is the lower append-adjunction comparison evaluated after
restriction; the latter is the upper append-adjunction comparison mapped
by upper restriction. Both are precisely the maps in the checked OLD
mate square. No independent loop comparison or gauge is chosen.

The consumer checks both colour parities on reordered mixed contents,
including parity-shifted evaluated regular probes and naturality on an
odd input map. These component comparisons are inputs to the canonical
mate and dotted-row compatibility statements.
