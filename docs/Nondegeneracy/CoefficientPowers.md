# Power and weight coefficient quotients

The finite reservoir of [SwappedDetectingFamily](SwappedDetectingFamily.md)
§2 uses powers of the positive even elementary coefficients. These powers
generate a different ideal from coefficient weight truncation. The bounds
below implement the comparison required by that construction without
identifying the two quotients or discarding exterior generators.

Work in the universal polynomial/exterior coefficient algebra `SYM D k`.
Let `J` be the existing ELV coefficient ideal at cutoff `N_i` and let
`T = coefficientPowerIdeal D k L` be the two-sided ideal generated by
`e_(i,r) ^ L_(i,r)`. Elementary variables are central and even. The original
ELV relations kill elementary variables with
`r ≥ elvElementaryRank D cutoff i`, including the prescribed extra odd
coefficient relation at even cutoff.

For finitely many colours, `elvPolynomialKeys` is the finite set of surviving
elementary variables. The explicit bound is

    R = Σ_(a in elvPolynomialKeys) (L_a − 1) weight(a) + number of odd colours.

`coefficientBasis_factor_elementary` factors a full coefficient monomial by
the entire power of any chosen elementary variable, leaving its exterior
factor in its original order. A monomial outside `J + T` therefore has no
polynomial support outside the finite key set and every exponent is below
its power cutoff. Its polynomial weight is bounded by the displayed sum;
the exterior weight is at most the number of odd colours. This proves
`coefficientBasis_mem_elv_sup_power` and, by linear spanning,
`coefficientIdeal_le_elv_sup_power`:

    W_(R+1) ⊆ J + T.

The other inclusion uses the actual numerical hypotheses. If
`N0 ≤ cutoff i` for every colour and
`N0 ≤ L_a * elementaryWeight a` for every elementary key, then
`elv_sup_power_le_coefficientIdeal` proves

    J + T ⊆ W_N0.

In the ELV coefficient quotient these give
`W_(R+1) ⊆ T ⊆ W_N0`. Consequently the coefficient quotient arrows point
from weighted `(R+1)` to the power quotient and then to weighted `N0`.
The module-category restriction directions are opposite. No uniform bound
on all numerical-weight fibres is asserted.

`elvPower_quotient_injective` proves bounded coefficient-window injectivity
directly for `SYM / (J + T)` when `d < N0`. The lower inclusion and the
existing independent coefficient basis supply the proof; no action
faithfulness or evaluation-kernel assumption is used. This injectivity
statement does not need the finite-colour hypothesis used to construct `R`.
