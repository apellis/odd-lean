# Constructed missing-colour coefficients on evaluated probes

Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) require the prescribed dotted-unit inverse
on the actual scalar-evaluated OLD module category.
`ELVProbeMissingCoefficients.probeEvaluatedMissingCoefficients` constructs
that coefficient record for every content missing the moving colour.
Its upper induction, restriction, ordinary adjunction and signed dot are
the existing standard evaluated choices. There is no supplied inverse or
extra action-family hypothesis.

The constructed raw OLD coefficients restrict componentwise to the full
evaluated subcategory. Their parities are retained. Total evaluation and
the forgetful functor preserve the finite sum, composition and identity,
so the original total and entry equations prove the evaluated equations.
The native component, total and cap comparisons retain every original
full-coefficient row, including the empty-window convention.

The exact producer and paired consumer pass strict checks with warnings as
errors and both compatibility settings. All 68 owned declarations pass the
axiom allowlist. The consumer covers both moving parities, nonempty mixed
missing-colour content, actual regular evaluated modules, odd input maps,
and an explicitly zero finite window. The actual evaluated top cap returns
the independently nonzero PBW vacuum vector after its chosen dotted unit;
nonzero extraction is not assumed from a comparison premise.

Transport from the standard upper representative to a chosen stored upper
loop is a separate even-chart application. These coefficients alone do
not construct the complete global local record or full multicolour action.
