# Supported mixed-mate weight coordinates

The Brundan–Ellis (1.12) weight mate has source `E_i F_j` and target
`F_j E_i`. `elvWeightMixedSource_obj` and `elvWeightMixedTarget_obj`
identify their existing product coordinates at an i-successor with the
literal four-corner content induction and restriction objects.
The common upper content is the j-successor of that output block.
`elvMixedUpper_content_eq` retains its equality to the other successor
order, and `elvInductionComponent_obj_of` permits an arbitrary literal
target with its predecessor certificate.

The producer and paired concrete consumer pass scoped strict checks and
an audit of all 25 owned declarations. The consumer uses shifted
representable modules and both mixed colour orders. Full CI remains the
integration gate. These are object equalities for the given weight
functors; the selected crossing/unit/counit comparison and supported
weight mate inverse remain open.
