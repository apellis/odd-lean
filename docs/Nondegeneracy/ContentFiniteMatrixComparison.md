# Prescribed finite matrices under the chosen content charts

DeformedMatrixComparison (4), (5) uses the entire unit-column and cap-row
matrices. `oldContentMatrixCharts_unitMatrix` and
`oldContentMatrixCharts_capMatrix` give their whole linear-map squares
through the selected E/F, F/E and finite parity-family charts.

The unit equation sums the prescribed entry equations over
`Option (Fin m)`. The cap equation compares their literal coordinate
projections. The whole family maps remain the selected coordinate products
`oldContentUnitFamilyEquiv` and `oldContentCapFamilyEquiv`; the proof does
not replace the finite forward maps with conjugate definitions.

Both equations hold for arbitrary input modules and output objects of
untruncated content `i :: ν`, all finite sizes including zero, and all
coend vectors. The paired mixed-content consumer ranges over both moving
parities and all finite sizes and audits every producer-owned declaration.
The existing old matrix inverses can now be transported through these
squares; their attachment to the given content components is the next
step. Coefficient-truncated content and absent-i contents remain separate.
