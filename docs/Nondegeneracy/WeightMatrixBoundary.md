# The absent-predecessor weight matrix boundary

For Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14), an output content missing i has zero
lower `E_i F_i` coordinate. `truncatedIndRes_subsingleton_of_not_mem`
proves this at every actual truncated word, for arbitrary input modules,
using content conservation of the coefficient arrow.
`truncatedIndRes_restriction_isZero` gives the whole zero module on that
content block. These statements concern the lower coordinate; they do
not assert vanishing of the following `F_i E_i` coordinate.

Under the source-required cutoff/highest-weight match,
`elvWeightHeight_nonpos_of_absent` shows that an absent predecessor can
occur only at nonpositive categorical height.
`elvWeightPredecessor_of_positive_height` therefore supplies the supported
content coordinates at every strictly positive categorical height.
The zero-height boundary still requires the count-zero unit inverse;
at negative height the following coordinate and all unit columns remain.

The producer and paired consumer pass scoped strict checks and an audit
of all eight owned declarations. The consumer covers both moving
parities, shifted representable modules, nonempty and genuinely empty
missing-colour contents at positive cutoff, and actual positive-height
predecessors. Full CI remains the integration gate. The finite matrix
inverse and its original-weight assembly remain separate.
