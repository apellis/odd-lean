# Sigma inversion on the actual module-category universes

The full ELV content-module products live in Π-SCat.{u,u,u+1} k. Their ambient object universe is u+2 and their hom/cell universes are u+1, while the original colours, weight group and coefficient ring remain in Type u.

The former Sigma/augmented-inversion section binders tied all four universes to u. Consequently a ProbeReservoir in the actual Π-SCat category could form its denominator/source-norm predicates but could not instantiate AugmentedInvertible with its own CrossCoeff.

This change separates the ambient bicategory object, hom and cell universes in the ten-module prerequisite chain: Supernatural.Leftward, LeftwardInverse, and eight Sigma modules. Every mathematical declaration body and hypothesis is preserved.

The paired consumer instantiates the existing weak augmented-inversion theorem at Π-SCat.{u,u,u+1} k with the original I, X and k. This is an interface compatibility check; it does not assume that a concrete full-ELV RouquierFamily has already been assembled. It also audits every declaration owned by all ten modules, including private/generated declarations.
