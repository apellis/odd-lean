# Deformed cyclotomic ideal sum

**Status: proposed written proof, pending independent review; not a Lean theorem.**
The ideal equality below does not depend on the proposed P balance or QP
calculation. Its application to a projective resolution does depend on those
inputs and on the specified right square-variable actions.

The source is KKO I, arXiv:1206.5933v2: Proposition 4.2 and Lemma 4.4
(p. 20), the cyclotomic definition in §7.1 (p. 35), and (8.1)–(8.6),
Lemma 8.2 (pp. 38–39). Lemma 8.2 there is obtained by reference to earlier
arguments, rather than by a printed reduction. Here is a parabolic PBW
proof with the normality substitution needed for ELV coefficients.

## 1. Ring, supports, and the two old quotients

Use finite compatible support in a symmetrizable Cartan superdatum, with
the homogeneous KKO parameters over a purely even commutative base k in
which 2 is invertible. Let A be the super tensor product of the coefficient
rings in `CyclotomicDeformationProof.md` §1. In particular, at an odd colour j,
with C(z)=z^m+χ₁z^(m−1)+…+χ_m,

- N_j=2m>0: f_j(x)=C(x²)−xd_j q(x²), q(z)=(C(z)−χ_m)/z,
  over k[χ₁,…,χ_m]⊗Λ(d_j)/(d_jχ_m);
- N_j=2m+1: f_j(x)=(x−d_j)C(x²), over k[χ₁,…,χ_m]⊗Λ(d_j);
- N_j=0: f_j=1, with no d_j.

Even-colour polynomials are monic with even coefficients. Products of odd
parameters of different colours are retained. No parameter is inverted.

Fix |β|=n, γ=β+α_i. Write H=A⊗ˢR(γ), R=A⊗ˢR(β),
e_R=e(β,i), e_L=e(i,β), and let ι_R,ι_L include R on positions
1,…,n and 2,…,n+1 respectively. These maps have units e_R,e_L,
not the unit of H. For n>0 put

    F_r = Σ_(μ of content γ) f_(μ_r)(x_r)e(μ),
    I = R F_old R,             B=R/I,
    J_R = H ι_R(I) = H F_1 ι_R(R),
    J_L = H ι_L(I) = H F_2 ι_L(R),
    I_new = H F_1 H.

Here F_old is the old first-position polynomial. Every expression defining
J_R ends on e_R and every expression defining J_L ends on e_L. The first
equalities include arbitrary old factors on BOTH sides of F_old; their
left old factor can be absorbed into H. This explains the second equalities
without asserting that the old cyclotomic ideal is principal.

The ordinary induced left modules are

    H e_R ⊗_R B = H e_R/J_R,
    H e_L ⊗_R B = H e_L/J_L.

These are quotient identities for tensor products, not a flatness argument.
Writing φ for total parity, including A, and ε=p(i)(N_i+p(β)), the
P-source is instead

    K₁=H e_L ⊗_(R,ι_L) Π^ε B,
    hι_L(b)⊗π^ε c = h⊗π^ε φ^ε(b)c.

Since I is homogeneous and φ(I)=I, its vanishing left submodule is still
J_L. Put K₀=H e_R/J_R and F=(H/I_new)e_R. At n=0 take R=B=A,
J_R=J_L=0 and e_R=e_L=e(i); there is no old first-position relation.

## 2. Normality in the first-strand parabolic algebra

Let D⊂H be generated by A, all dots and idempotents, and τ₂,…,τ_n.
It preserves the first colour. Set E_j=Σ_(μ₁=j)e(μ), D_j=E_j D E_j,
and r_j=p(j)N_j modulo 2. In D_j write f=f_j(x₁)E_j.
There is an automorphism ρ_j satisfying

    b f = f ρ_j(b)                       (b∈D_j).             (N)

At even j it is the identity. At odd j with positive cutoff it is given by

    ρ_j(x₁)=x₁−2d_j,
    ρ_j(c)=(-1)^(r_j|c|)c                 (homogeneous c∈A),
    ρ_j(x_a e(μ))=(-1)^(r_j p(μ_a))x_a e(μ)       (a≥2),
    ρ_j(τ_a e(μ))=(-1)^(r_j p(μ_a)p(μ_(a+1)))τ_a e(μ) (a≥2),
    ρ_j(e(μ))=e(μ).

At cutoff zero take ρ_j=1. The formulas on A and the old tail are the
TOTAL parity automorphism to power r_j, not parity only on affine dots.
The first dot supercommutes with that tail. Its translate x₁−2d_j has the
same supercommutations, since d_j is an odd supercentral coefficient.
The first dot and every odd coefficient still anticommute after applying
these formulas. Thus the formulas preserve the presentation of D_j.
For even N_j the inverse translates x₁ by +2d_j and fixes A and the tail;
for odd N_j the displayed automorphism is involutive, because it also
negates d_j. This proves invertibility without a faithful representation.

For (N), tail dots, tail crossings and coefficients supercommute with f
with sign (-1)^(r_j times their parity). Termwise this sign is the sum of
the dot and coefficient exponents, since every term of f has parity r_j.
The only non-supercentral generator is x₁. Its relation is

    x₁ f = f(x₁−2d_j).

At even cutoff the difference of these two sides before imposing the
coefficient relation is 2χ_m d_j; it is zero in A precisely as required
by d_jχ_m=0. At odd cutoff it follows from f=(x₁−d_j)C(x₁²).
Consequently (N) holds for products and sums. In particular,

    f D_j = D_j f,

using ρ_j inverse in one direction. This parabolic normality, NOT centrality
in H and NOT transport through τ₁, is the needed replacement for the
undeformed argument. It also retains all other odd parameters.

## 3. Affine cosets and both ideal inclusions

Put T_a=τ₁…τ_a for 0≤a≤n, with T₀=1. Every permutation w∈S_(n+1)
has a unique decomposition

    w=v(s₁…s_a),   v∈⟨s₂,…,s_n⟩,
    length(w)=length(v)+a.

Choose the PBW reduced word for w to be exactly this concatenation.
Proposition 4.2 therefore gives the spanning decomposition

    H = Σ_(a=0)^n D T_a.                                  (C)

More explicitly, a PBW monomial x^u τ_w e(μ) becomes
x^u τ_v T_a e(μ); insert e((s₁…s_a)μ) before T_a. Its coefficient
x^u τ_v lies in D. This is an affine PBW statement before imposing any
cyclotomic relation. Extending the explicit PBW direct-sum isomorphism to
A needs no flatness of A or of B. Choosing these reduced words introduces
NO braid correction. If a different reduced-word system is used, braid
moves give terms with strictly smaller crossing length; induction on that
nonnegative integer gives the same spanning result, retaining the
polynomial braid corrections. No degree sweep or cancellation is involved.

Now take an arbitrary generator h F_1 h' of I_new. Expand h' by (C),
then split its D factor into first-colour components. In each component,

    h F_1 b_j T_a = h f_j b_j T_a
                 = h ρ_j^(-1)(b_j) f_j T_a ∈ H F_1 T_a.

Here b_j is an arbitrary element of D_j. The formula uses (N) once on the entire parabolic factor;
it does not move F_1 through a crossing meeting position 1. Hence

    I_new ⊆ Σ_(a=0)^n H F_1 T_a.

The reverse inclusion holds because T_a∈H and I_new is the two-sided
ideal generated by F_1. We have proved the deformed analogue of part (i):

    H F_1 H = Σ_(a=0)^n H F_1 T_a.                        (IS1)

Right-multiply (IS1) by e_R. If a<n, T_a e_R lies in ι_R(R):
it involves only the old positions 1,…,n and fixes the last i-strand.
Thus every such summand lies in J_R. Conversely J_R⊂I_new e_R,
and H F_1 T_n e_R⊂I_new e_R. Both inclusions give

    I_new e_R = J_R + H F_1 T_n e_R.                      (IS2)

The longest term has exact left support e_L because T_n moves the last
i-strand to the first position:

    W=F_1 T_n e_R=f_i(x₁)T_n e_R ∈ e_L H e_R.

For n=0, (IS1) follows directly from first-dot normality and (IS2) reads
H f_i=0+H f_i, as it should. Thus all cutoffs, including zero, are covered.
The reduction terminates after a finite PBW expansion and one normalizer
application per parabolic coefficient. Its only deformation correction is
the explicitly retained translation ±2d_j (with total parity at odd cutoff).

## 4. Cokernel and the conditional projective sequence

Independently of a map P, (IS2) identifies the kernel of the canonical
surjection K₀→F as

    ker(K₀→F) = (I_new e_R)/J_R = (J_R+H W)/J_R.          (K)

Assume now the proposed old-generator P balance and old-ideal descent of
`DeformedGlobalQP.md` §4, so that

    P(h⊗π^ε b)=hW⊗b

is a defined left H-linear, right B-linear map with the stated parity
shift. Its image lies in (K) because W lies in I_new e_R. Conversely
P(h e_L⊗π^ε 1)=hW modulo J_R, so its image contains all of (K).
Consequently the balanced-map input gives

    K₁ --P--> K₀ --> (H/I_new)e_R --> 0

exact, and coker P is this actual induction corner, not merely an abstract
module of the same rank. No injectivity or source flatness is used here.

For an odd moving colour put s=t², acting by the moving first-dot square
on K₁ and the last-dot square on K₀ and F. For an even moving colour one
may use s=t. To conclude that

    0 --> K₁ --P--> K₀ --> F --> 0

is a projective right B[s]-resolution, the remaining inputs must be
established, not inferred from (IS2): the specified square-right actions
and P-linearity, projectivity of K₀,K₁ over B[s] from affine coset
coordinates, and the all-prefix QP formula with its injective monic
moving-dot multiplier and the correctly typed Q target. The proposed QP
and P balance are undergoing separate review; this document does not
assert that review passed. With those inputs P is injective, giving the
displayed resolution. With the additional central monic s-annihilator
and the central-polynomial projectivity argument, F is right B-projective.
None of this asserts the full multicolour action, opposite-side
projectivity, or Rouquier inversion.
