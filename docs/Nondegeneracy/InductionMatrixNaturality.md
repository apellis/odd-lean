# Input-module naturality of the matrix fields

The matrix fields in Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) are constructed from a
single induction–restriction adjunction. `dotEnd_power_supernatural`,
`unitDotColumn_supernatural`, and `capDotRow_supernatural` show that the
given dot powers and the ordinary unit/counit fields have their specified
signed naturality in every homogeneous input-module map.

`orderedMateComponent_supernatural` proves the same assertion for the
given cup-crossing-cap mate. The crossing retains its prescribed minus.
The proof composes the adjunction unit, negative flattened crossing, and
induced adjunction counit as supernatural families. No inverse or
naturality hypothesis on the resulting mate is supplied.

Canonical source or target parity shifts turn these families into even
natural transformations, so their naturality includes inhomogeneous
input-module maps. The paired consumer applies these assertions to the
selected weighted truncated append, both colours, every dot power,
canonical odd module parity maps, and the vacuum. Its shifted-family
examples permit arbitrary input maps. All producer declarations are
checked against the standard three-axiom allowlist.

These are the individual fields needed for the input-functor assembly
of the weighted finite matrix isomorphisms. The whole finite-family
assembly is a separate step.
