# Matrix counts on an original-weight fibre

DeformedActionAssembly §§1, 6 uses highest cutoff weight Λ and weight
sum(α)−Λ. When cutoff(i)=h_i(Λ), the old-content matrix height is −h_i(x)
on every content of weight x. The unit and cap counts are respectively
(−h_i(x)).toNat and h_i(x).toNat. These are finite counts shared across
the entire fibre; no root independence or finite-fibre hypothesis is used.

`ELVWeightMatrixHeight` proves this directly from the defining weight
identity. Its consumer uses two odd colours with dependent roots: the
empty content and [false,true] belong to the same original-weight fibre,
and every content in that fibre has unit count three and cap count zero.
The paired consumer audits every owned declaration against the standard
axiom allowlist. Original-weight matrix assembly still needs its selected
E/F and field comparisons; uniform counts alone supply no inverse.
