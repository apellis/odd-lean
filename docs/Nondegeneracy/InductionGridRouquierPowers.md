# The dotted Rouquier fields of the induction grid

Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) uses ordered powers of the prescribed dot
along the ordinary right unit and right counit. The whole input-module
families `inductionModuleUnitPower_eq` and `inductionModuleCapPower_eq`
identify those exact fields with `gridUnitColumnTransformation` and
`gridCapRowTransformation` for every power. `inductionModuleDot_total`
retains the same given module dot.

The consumer instantiates actual old empty/one-letter content in both
moving parities and pairs the finite Rouquier unit fields with the
constructed prescribed inverse rows. No inverse compatibility is assumed.
The right counit used here remains distinct from the inverse-defined raw
left cap and the normalized left-adjunction counit.
