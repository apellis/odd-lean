# Input naturality of the prescribed weighted entries

The input-module step of Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) retains the given
weighted matrix fields. `weightedUnitMatrixSummandFunctor` and
`weightedCapMatrixSummandFunctor` act on their literal canonical parity
coordinates. Their object equations identify each coordinate with the
existing prescribed matrix family.

`weightedUnitMatrixEntryNatTrans` and `weightedCapMatrixEntryNatTrans`
are natural transformations on the entire weighted supermodule category.
The `_app` equations identify their components with the specified unit or
cap entry and the explicit endpoint equality. The principal entry uses
the prescribed negative-crossing mate; all other entries use the ordinary
unit/dot column or dot/counit row without rescaling.

The generic datum-level mate family is proved before specializing the
truncated quotient, preserving its precise module endpoints. Canonical
parity shifts then make every entry even in the input-module category.
This gives naturality for odd and inhomogeneous input maps.

The paired consumer tests every Option/Fin slot, both moving parities,
zero-count principal entries, odd canonical parity maps of an occupied
connected two-colour module, arbitrary input maps, and the vacuum.
Every producer declaration is audited against the standard allowlist.
Whole finite matrices and their input-functor inverses are assembled in
the following step.
