# The selected finite inverse at stored probe representatives

The constructors `probeAppendToInvPos` and `probeAppendToInvNeg` in
`ELVProbeAppendToLocalInverse` supply Brundan–Ellis (1.13)–(1.14) at
arbitrary prescribed content lists. They take no inverse or local-record
premise. The height remains minus the OLD intermediate content height,
so the positive record uses dotted counits and the negative record uses
dotted units.

The chosen mate and dotted rows coincide with the transported standard
fields by the checked mate and row reindexing squares. `copyMaps` replaces
these equal forward maps while retaining every inverse entry literally.
The four `probeAppendToInvPos_f'`, `probeAppendToInvPos_g'`,
`probeAppendToInvNeg_f'` and `probeAppendToInvNeg_g'` equalities are `rfl`,
including row indices outside the finite sum. Thus no additional inverse
choice, gauge or zero extension is introduced.

The consumer checks both parities and height directions on pure and
reordered mixed contents, finite off-diagonal products, all-index entry
retention and the height-zero overlap with both inverse orientations.
Memberships and dot slides of the complete local record are supplied
separately by the selected fields and the OLD affine relations.
