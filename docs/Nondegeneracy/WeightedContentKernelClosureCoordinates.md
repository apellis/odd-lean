# Closure coefficients at arbitrary stored content

For the G8 coefficient attachment,
`weightedContentUnitClosureValue_evaluate` and
`weightedContentCapClosureValue_evaluate` identify the literal weighted
local closure with the action of a represented source endomorphism, at
every object of arbitrary stored content and on every module vector.

`weightedContentUnitClosureCoefficient` and
`weightedContentCapClosureCoefficient` are constructed from the original
raw OLD unit–principal-inverse–cap closures, applied to the identity and
then mapped to the truncated quotient. The construction first uses a
literal word and then transports its typed endomorphism along the stored
content equality. A generic dependent content recursor and its abstract
canonical reduction equation perform this transport before specializing
to the inverse coefficient. The canonical evaluations compose the
existing OLD comparison and the quotient-map comparison as equalities.
They retain the same selected inverse, unit and cap.

`weightedContentUnitClosureCoefficient_degree` and
`weightedContentCapClosureCoefficient_degree` place these endomorphisms
in source integer degree `2 dᵢ (r+s+1)`, under precisely the existing
symmetrizer and homogeneous-parameter assumptions. At a literal word this
is `ELVKernelClosureDegree` followed by preservation of degree under the
quotient; the same abstract content transport gives the arbitrary-content
statement. No inverse or module-grading compatibility premise is added.
