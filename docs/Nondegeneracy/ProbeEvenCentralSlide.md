# The even vacuum slide

`ELVProbeEvenCentralSlide.evaluatedMixedBend_even_slide` targets
SwappedVacuumNonpositiveBubbles §1 (1) for an even moving colour inside
arbitrary mixed data. Its maps are the actual shifted probe dot, evaluated
reservoir dot, finite mixed bend, prescribed pre-cap and post unit.

For the negative finite geometric series `S`, telescoping gives
`S Y - X S = t - X⁻ⁿ t Yⁿ`, where `t` is the cap followed by the unit.
Choose a common exponent above both the induced and restricted reservoir
dot nilpotence bounds. The boundary term is zero. This gives the same
signed slide interface used by polynomial-cap uniqueness, now with
Koszul sign one. No slide identity is assumed.

The concrete consumer has a connected mixed datum and a nonzero tensor
input carrying the other colour's exterior coefficient. The even
polynomial-cap and chosen inverse-row comparison remain subsequent steps.

The producer and concrete consumer pass strict checks; all 18 owned
declarations are audited against the standard axioms.
