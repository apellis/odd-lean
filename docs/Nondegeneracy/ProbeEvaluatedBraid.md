crossing. The theorem `probeEvaluatedEqualCross_braid` restricts
`oldContentInductionCross_braid` along the full inclusions of scalar-evaluated
module categories. Its terms use the three consecutive actual evaluated
induction functors and the chosen negative crossing on each adjacent pair.
No braid or generator-comparison premise is imposed.

`probeEvaluatedBraidPrefix` maps the chosen first-pair crossing by the third
induction, and `probeEvaluatedBraidSuffix` evaluates the independently chosen

lemmas identify these maps directly. `probeEvaluatedBraid` states the
resulting equality of whole super natural transformations on the threefold

rather than imposing an unsigned coefficient comparison.

The consumer uses the rational two-colour fixture and both parity choices,
on a mixed-content probe, its parity shift, and the empty-content probe.

components' naturality on an odd input map. The statement concerns the
successive evaluated content inductions; comparison with arbitrary weight
representatives is separate.
