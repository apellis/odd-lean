# Deformed intertwiner transport and the square-variable resolution

**Status: proposed written proof, not a Lean theorem.** The result proved below
is the signed transport of the full cyclotomic relation through an arbitrary
intertwiner chain, including its repeated-odd cancellation step. The complete
balanced multicolour resolution is not claimed constructed. In particular,
this does not upgrade the status of ActionReview.md's balanced P argument.

## Coefficients and source boundary

Use a finite compatible symmetrizable Cartan superdatum and homogeneous KKO
parameters, over a purely even commutative ring k with 2 invertible. All
leading Q coefficients used for monicity are units. The coefficient algebra
A is the super tensor product of the even-colour polynomial parameter rings
and the following odd-colour rings. With C(z)=z^m+χ₁z^(m−1)+…+χ_m,

- N=2m>0: A_i=k[χ₁,…,χ_m]⊗Λ(d_i)/(d_iχ_m),
  q(z)=(C(z)−χ_m)/z, f_i(x)=C(x²)−xd_i q(x²);
- N=2m+1: A_i=k[χ₁,…,χ_m]⊗Λ(d_i), f_i(x)=(x−d_i)C(x²);
- N=0: f_i=1, with no d_i.

The d_i are independent odd generators; products d_i d_j are retained.
No localization of χ_m is made. The degrees are those in
CyclotomicDeformationProof.md §1. The base A is not replaced by a standalone
exterior ring. Nothing below assumes A-flatness of a cyclotomic block.

The source comparison is KKO I, arXiv:1206.5933v2, §7.1, Lemmas 7.2–7.3,
§8 (in particular (8.13), Proposition 8.4 and Theorem 8.5), and §9's
Mackey diagram; KKO II, arXiv:1303.1916v2, §8.3; ELV,
arXiv:2203.14153v2, (3.24)–(3.27) and (4.15)–(4.16).
KKO's coefficients at odd colours have even index; ELV supplies the new
odd coefficients and the even-cutoff relation. Kang–Kashiwara,
arXiv:1102.4677v4, Lemma 4.18 is the central-polynomial projectivity lemma.
These are source inputs, not citations for a multicolour ELV theorem.

## 1. The deformation transport lemma

Let u be a homogeneous product of KKO intertwiners g_a, supported on a fixed
word, inducing the permutation w. Write h=|u|. For a strand r of colour j,
the affine intertwiner identities and super coefficient extension give

    x_(w(r)) u = (−1)^(h p(j)) u x_r,
    c u = (−1)^(h |c|) u c                  (c homogeneous in A).

If F_j(x)=Σ_r x^r c_r has parity p(j)N, so
|c_r|=p(j)(N−r), then

    F_j(x_(w(r₀))) u = (−1)^(h p(j)N) u F_j(x_(r₀)).       (1)

Here r₀ is the strand index, not the polynomial exponent. Proof: the sign
on the r-th summand is
h p(j)r+h|c_r|=h p(j)N. It is independent of r, so the sum transports.
This proves (1) term by term over A, with no coefficient inversion and no
flatness assumption. It applies to every colour in the chain, not only to
the moving colour.

For the new even-cutoff term this check is substantive: transporting x d_i
has **two** signs. At an odd intertwiner they cancel. Transporting x and
leaving d_i fixed would replace C(x²)−xd_iq(x²) by C(x²)+xd_iq(x²), and
would not transport the old cyclotomic ideal. At odd cutoff the entire
polynomial has sign (−1)^h, not a term-dependent sign. Thus the parity
automorphism in the balance must include A, not just the affine algebra.

Consequently an old relation F_j at the permuted strand kills the transported
vector in the corresponding balanced quotient: (1) places it on the old
algebra factor. This is the deformation-specific input in KKO's repeated-
colour induction in Theorem 8.5, where an expression is moved into the
left ideal generated by the old first-position cyclotomic relation.
The existing all-Hom intertwiner balance alone does not prove this assertion
for the super coefficient extension: the coefficient sign calculation above
is also needed.

## 2. Repeated-odd cancellation, without cyclotomic freeness

Here is the cancellation step with its ambient module specified. Let x,y be
two adjacent odd i-dots, τ their crossing, and set

    Δ=y²−x²,   S=y²+x²,
    g=Δ((y−x)−Δτ)=(τΔ−(y−x))Δ.

Take the affine two-strand algebra over A, balanced on the right against its
old second-strand cyclotomic algebra B=A⟨y⟩/(f_i(y)). Denote this module by M.
Thus a rightmost old relation f_i(y) is zero in M, whereas the moving first
dot x has **not** been cyclotomically specialized. Put R_y=A⟨y⟩. Use the
right PBW coordinates with crossings on the left and dots on the right:

    H = ⨁_(ε∈{0,1}, a≥0) τ^ε x^a R_y,
    M = H ⊗_(R_y) B = ⨁_(ε∈{0,1}, a≥0) τ^ε x^a B.

Tensoring this explicit direct sum does not require A-flatness or
A-projectivity of B; no unsplit exact sequence is being tensored.

By (1), f_i(x)g=(−1)^N g f_i(y)=0 in M. Using the second expression for g,

    (f_i(x)τΔ−f_i(x)(y−x))Δ=0.

Right multiplication by Δ is well defined: x² commutes with R_y and y² is
central in R_y, so it preserves the balanced quotient. In the above coordinates,

    (τ^ε x^a ⊗ b) Δ = τ^ε x^a ⊗ by² − τ^ε x^(a+2) ⊗ b.

Neither square is moved across τ^ε, so no crossing terms mix. A nonzero
finite-support vector has a largest x-exponent a; at exponent a+2 its image
has coefficient −b_(ε,a), nonzero for some ε. Right multiplication by Δ is
therefore injective, even when B has zero divisors. Equivalently its leading
action in square degree s=x² is −s in each crossing/parity component. Hence

    f_i(x)τΔ=f_i(x)(y−x).

The nilHecke identity τg=τΔ(y−x) now gives the exact quotient identity

    f_i(x)τg = f_i(x)(y−x)² = f_i(x)(x²+y²).               (2)

The final equality uses xy=−yx. Neither f_i(x) nor d_i was cancelled.
In particular the factor in QP is S, not the intertwiner square
−Δ²S. Formula (2) holds for the full even-cutoff ELV relation with χ_m
present, as well as for odd cutoff. The transport lemma (1) supplies the
same relation-killing step after a multicolour prefix; the square-variable
cancellation takes place in the analogous first-dot polynomial module.

## 3. Balanced objects and the remaining global identity

For clarity the precise objects to be constructed are as follows. Put
H=A⊗ˢR(β+α_i), R=A⊗ˢR(β), and B=R/(old cyclotomic ideal).
Let ξ:R→H be inclusion on positions 2,…,n+1 and let φ be total parity,
including coefficients. Set η=p(i)p(β), ε=p(i)(N_i+p(β)). Then

    K₀ = H e(β,i) ⊗_R B,
    K₁ = H e(i,β) ⊗_R Π^ε B,
    K₁′= H e(i,β) ⊗_R Π^η B.

The last two tensor products use ξ on the first factor; Π^a B has left
action b·π^a c=π^a φ^a(b)c and the unchanged right action. Equivalently,
the relation is hξ(b)⊗π^a c=h⊗π^a φ^a(b)c. Integer shifts make P of
degree zero when required; its unshifted degree is (α_i|2Λ−β).
With T=τ₁…τ_n and U=g_n…g₁ the formulas are

    P(h⊗π^ε b)=h f_i(x₁)T⊗b,
    Q(h⊗b)=hU⊗π^η b.

The old-generator P balance and ideal-sum equality are still separate
obligations. Formula (1) supplies the additional cyclotomic transport for Q;
it does not replace those P calculations or construct the tensor maps in
Lean. The target for the global QP calculation, in first-position quotient
coordinates, is

    f_i(x₁) T U e(i,ν)
      = f_i(x₁) ∏_(ν_a≠i) Q_(i,ν_a)(x₁,x_(a+1))
                  ∏_(ν_a=i odd) (x₁²+x_(a+1)²) e(i,ν).   (3)

The quotient on the right is the one balancing the old positions 2,…,n+1
against B. There is no repeated-even factor. In KKO's signed t-coordinate,
the corresponding multiplier is

    (−1)^(p(i)N_i p(β)) f_i(t)
        ∏_(ν_a≠i) Q_(i,ν_a)(t,x_a)
        ∏_(ν_a=i odd) (t²+x_a²),

with the comparison between K₁ and K₁′ applying φ_i^N_i to b as in
Theorem 8.5. Equation (2) proves the repeated-odd elementary step of (3);
(1) proves its coefficient-sensitive transport. A complete all-prefix
induction and balanced-map assembly is not asserted here.

## 4. Why the central-square argument remains available

At even odd-colour cutoff the correct relation d_iχ_m=0 gives

    x f_i=f_i(x−2d_i),
    (C(x²)+xd_iq(x²)) f_i=C(x²)².

The first identity fails without that relation: its defect
x f_i−f_i(x−2d_i) is 2χ_m d_i. This is the exact local obstruction for an
independent polynomial/exterior coefficient ring; it is zero over A.
At odd cutoff the normalizer acts by parity on coefficients and sends
x to x−2d_i; it does not fix x. Also f_i²=x²C(x²)².
At even cutoff the normalizer fixes coefficients and its inverse sends
x to x+2d_i. These normalizers both fix x². In particular one must not
copy the original KKO right-t linearity into the deformed resolution.

It is enough for projectivity to work over B[s], s=t², at an odd moving
colour. P is s-linear: applying the shifted first-dot relation twice
removes the d_i term because (t−2d_i)²=t². The affine polynomial extension
is free on 1,t over B[s]; restriction therefore preserves projectivity of
K₀ and K₁. The displayed norms give first-position monic annihilators with
even central coefficients. KKO Lemma 7.3 propagates such annihilators; the
propagation uses even square polynomials and the Q relations, so its
coefficients remain in the even parameter subring. No cyclotomic basis is
needed for this step.

Once P balance, right exactness and (3) are established globally, the monic
QP multiplier makes P injective in its polynomial source module. The exact
sequence is then a length-one projective B[s]-resolution of the induction
corner F. A central monic s-annihilator of F allows Kang–Kashiwara Lemma
4.18 to conclude that F is B-projective. This implication assumes neither
A-freeness of B nor A-freeness of F. It is not a proof of those missing
balanced identities, and is not being used backwards to justify them.

Thus there is no failed ELV transport relation in this calculation. The
bounded result is (1) and the cancellation (2), with the exact global
formula (3) and the unfinished assembly explicitly separated. Full
multicolour projectivity, opposite-side projectivity and the specified
Rouquier inversion remain unproved here.
