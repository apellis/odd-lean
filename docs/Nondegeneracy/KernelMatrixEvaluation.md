# Whole kernel-matrix evaluation

This is an unverified draft of the whole cap-matrix comparison in DeformedMatrixComparison §4. It composes the checked arbitrary-module quotient coordinates with the actual coefficient-kernel map, and compares that map with the prescribed finite categorical matrix. Each slot is tested after its canonical parity coordinate; the entry calculations are the existing mate and ordinary counit/dot evaluations.

The draft is not exported. Kernel/source endpoint normalizations remain under construction in ELVKernelEntryEndpoints; no whole-matrix equality or arbitrary-module inverse is claimed by this draft. The unit-column matrix, concrete consumer, strict source check and full exact-head CI are further obligations. The checked source/target coordinate and entry-evaluation results retain their separate claim boundaries.
