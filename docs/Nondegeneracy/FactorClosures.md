# Native inverse contractions as coefficient actions

SwappedHomInversion §4, equations (7)–(9), requires identifying the native
reservoir contractions with source coefficients. `postCapInverse_closure`
and `postCupInverse_closure` express the prescribed dotted cup, chosen
principal inverse and dotted cap as `postCoeff` of the corresponding
chosen factor closure. `postCapInverse_boundary` and
`postCupInverse_boundary` give the same equation for every finite inverse
column/row contraction and `W.loc.boundaryPos` / `boundaryNeg`.

The proof uses the existing literal source, target and unit charts.
Intermediate inverse chart pairs cancel; the remaining composite is
post-whiskering of the factor coefficient. No inverse, equality, grading
or nilpotence hypothesis is added. The equations hold before evaluation
and therefore remain available to arbitrary additive evaluation functors.

The producer and paired consumer pass strict scoped checks with warnings
as errors and both compatibility settings. The consumer audits all six
owned declarations against the standard axiom allowlist.
The consumer constructs the odd rank-one ELV reservoir family, uses both
nonempty height-sign windows and both height-zero orientations, and retains
a nonzero odd vector in a positive-content module. Actual coefficient
ideal membership still requires the chosen source degree and its content
projection. Mixed-bend separation and the residual matrix remain separate.
